Open-weight AI is having its Kubernetes moment (1 minute read)

The author argues that open-weight AI models are following the same path as Kubernetes: once an open, customizable platform becomes an industry's center of gravity, no single closed vendor can match the combined rate of innovation around it. Drawing on his experience co-founding Mesosphere, which Kubernetes disrupted, he contends that open weights turn a model into a neutral substrate that engineers, cloud providers, and enterprises can all extend. The strategic takeaway is that the US should compete in this ecosystem rather than wall itself off.

UK AISI / Caisi Preliminary Assessment of Kimi K3's Cyber Capabilities (1 minute read)

UK AISI and CAISI released a joint preliminary evaluation of Moonshot AI's Kimi K3, focusing on the model's offensive cyber capabilities ahead of its open-weight release on July 27. Kimi K3 was scored only on ExploitBench, Carnegie Mellon's 41-task benchmark measuring end-to-end exploit development, giving it a wider confidence interval than U.S. models assessed across more domains. The U.S. closed-weight models were tested with system-level safeguards disabled to measure maximal capability.
