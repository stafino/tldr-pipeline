Anthropic’s Claude Cowork could escape its local VM and read credentials on a Mac (1 minute read)

Security researchers at Accomplish AI demonstrated that Anthropic's Claude Cowork could escape its local VM sandbox and read files across the host Mac, including SSH keys and cloud credentials. The attack, dubbed SharedRoot, exploited a Linux kernel privilege escalation flaw (CVE-2026-46331) to gain guest root, then reached the host through a writable filesystem mount, exposing roughly 500,000 macOS users running local sessions.

The author traces a four-layer supply chain: card and account merchants forging virtual cards that pass Western billing checks, account pools aggregating hundreds of stolen or bulk-registered accounts, and relays that wrap them in a clean Chinese-language product. One tracked package sold $3,333 of Anthropic credit for roughly $56.
