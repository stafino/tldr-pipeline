Here’s what Anthropic found when it turned Mythos loose on encryption algorithms (1 minute read)

Anthropic used Claude Mythos Preview to find the strongest cryptographic weaknesses it has identified to date, including a flaw in HAWK, a post-quantum digital signature scheme under NIST review. The attack exploits a nontrivial automorphism in HAWK's lattice structure that halves its effective key strength, forcing key sizes to double, while a second attack on a seven-round version of AES eliminated a 256-value lookup step. Neither flaw affects software in use today.

Kimi K3 Architecture Overview and Notes (1 minute read)

Kimi K3 is a 2.8T-parameter open-weight model, now the largest available, and this post walks through its architecture as a scaled-up production version of last year's Kimi Linear. The main additions are LatentMoE, which down-projects large linear layers similar to multi-head latent attention, and a broader push toward inference efficiency shared with Nemotron 3 and DeepSeek V4. K3 also drops RoPE entirely for NoPE across all layers, making it the first frontier-level model to do so, and adds native multimodal support.

Discovering Cryptographic Weaknesses with Claude (1 minute read)

Anthropic researchers used Claude Mythos Preview to discover new mathematical attacks against two cryptographic algorithms: an improved attack that significantly weakens HAWK, a post-quantum digital signature scheme, and a new method against round-reduced AES, the most widely used symmetric cipher. Both are substantial research advances that do not currently affect any production systems, marking a shift from Claude finding implementation bugs to flaws in the algorithms themselves.

Kimi Linear: An Expressive, Efficient Attention Architecture (2025) (1 minute read)

Kimi Linear is a hybrid linear attention architecture that outperforms full attention across short-context, long-context, and RL scaling regimes, built on Kimi Delta Attention (KDA), a gated linear attention module extending Gated DeltaNet. The 48B-parameter model with 3B active parameters cuts KV cache usage by up to 75% and reaches up to 6x decoding throughput at 1M context, with open-sourced kernels, vLLM implementations, and checkpoints.
