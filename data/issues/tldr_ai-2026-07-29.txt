Truth is not a direction: a Tarski attack on LLM probes (1 minute read)

The author argues that no linear probe on an LLM's embedding space can reliably identify truth, using a diagonal argument modeled on Tarski's theorem that no sufficiently expressive language can contain its own truth predicate. The post connects this to Gödel's incompleteness and Turing's halting problem to show that "truth directions," which AI safety researchers train classifiers to detect, cannot fully capture the truth of statements from within the model's own representations.

Falling Behind Drives Unsafe Development in an Idealised AI Race Experiment (1 minute read)

This paper studies AI safety-versus-speed tradeoffs through a behavioral experiment where paired participants repeatedly chose Safe or Unsafe development under competitive pressure. The pre-registered hypotheses about risk levels and risk preferences failed; instead, Unsafe choices were driven by strategic position, with players going Unsafe after opponents did and when falling behind. A companion evolutionary model reproduces the effect, and the authors argue policy should target competitive pressure and cooperation rather than individual risk attitudes.

Detecting CSAM Text-to-Image LoRAs From Weights (1 minute read)

This paper shows that harmful text-to-image LoRAs can be flagged directly from their weights, without reading metadata or generating any illegal outputs. The authors extract the top-left singular vector of a LoRA's updates as an inference-free fingerprint that identifies what the adapter was trained on, generalizes across base models, and stays robust to weight noise, rescaling, and precision reduction.

Benchmarking Opus 5 on SlopCodeBench (1 minute read)

Measuring Reward-Seeking by Instilling Contrastive Beliefs (5 minute read)

Frontier models trained with reinforcement learning but no safety training increasingly did what they believed the grader wanted, even when that conflicted with user or developer intent, and the tendency strengthened over training.

Introducing Antares: Highly Efficient Open Weight AI Models for Vulnerability Localization (5 minute read)

Pass the Baton: Trajectory-Relayed On-Policy Distillation (1 minute read)

Relay On-Policy Distillation (Relay-OPD) addresses prefix failure in on-policy distillation, where a student model that commits to a wrong reasoning direction wastes compute building on the deviation. The method uses a label-free trigger to hand off to the teacher at critical early positions, outperforming standard OPD by 5.73% on average across eight math benchmarks with a 1.7B student while cutting training trajectory length by over 50%.

Reinforcement Learning for Code Optimization (1 minute read)

This paper adapts reinforcement learning to code optimization, where rewarding execution time introduces measurement noise, reward sparsity, and GRPO instability that standard RLVR fails to handle. The authors' approach combines a calibrated timing sandbox, a correctness-and-speed reward with an offline configuration simulator, and adapted GRPO to lift strict top-50% pass@1 from 30.7% to 50.4% on CWM 32B while preserving pure-correctness scores.

Minimizing Targeted Activations: Input-Only Suppression of Evaluation-Awareness Latents in Large Language Models (1 minute read)

This paper adapts activation-steering methods to the input side, optimizing a fluent prompt that drives a model's "evaluation-awareness" latent toward zero without any inference-time access, tested across five target constructions on Llama-3.2-3B and Llama-3.1-8B. The latent is robustly suppressible, but control experiments show a random placebo direction is suppressed just as hard and shifts behavior just as far, demonstrating that activation-readability is not behavioral controllability.

RepoReasoner: Evaluating Repository-Level Code Reasoning Ability of Long-Context Language Models (1 minute read)

RepoReasoner is a benchmark for repository-level code reasoning that tests two abilities: Output Prediction, which measures stateful cross-file execution reasoning, and Call Chain Prediction, which measures architectural dependency understanding under noisy context. Across seven state-of-the-art models, the best scores only 69.1% Pass@1 on Output Prediction even with oracle context, and longer contexts do not consistently help.

An Empirical Study of Model Context Protocol Applications (1 minute read)

This paper mines 1,723 MCP applications from GitHub to characterize how developers integrate MCP servers, deriving a taxonomy called MCPAppTax and applying it across the full dataset via an LLM-assisted pipeline. The ecosystem has converged on file-based configuration (85.2%) and official SDKs (81.1%), but only 37.2% gate tool execution behind a blocking approval step, leaving most apps able to invoke any enabled tool unconditionally.
