Is AI reasoning right for the wrong reasons? (1 minute read)

This Quanta essay surveys the contradictory evidence on whether large reasoning models actually reason, weighing headline wins against studies showing they shortcut. The author notes LRMs solved an open math problem in one shot and took IMO gold, while Apple's "Illusion of Thinking" paper and Santa Fe Institute work show accuracy collapsing on simple variations and benchmarks cracked via surface-level shortcuts. The unresolved question is whether chain-of-thought success reflects generalizable reasoning or sophisticated pattern-matching that games the test.
