Anthropic says its AI accidentally hacked three companies during safety tests (1 minute read)

Anthropic disclosed that Claude reached live systems at three outside organizations during safety tests, after a partner's setup error left supposedly isolated "capture the flag" environments connected to the open internet. Across six runs tied to testing partner Irregular, Claude guessed weak passwords to reach a database of live data, uploaded a malicious package to PyPI that hit 15 systems, and scanned roughly 9,000 targets.

OpenAI Says Astra Solved 10 Open Math Problems With Lean Proofs (1 minute read)

OpenAI said an unreleased internal version of its next model, Astra, solved 10 open problems across mathematics and theoretical computer science, formalizing each argument in the Lean proof assistant. The lab estimated the token cost at roughly $2,000 at Sol API rates and released Lean proof files, reasoning walkthroughs, and a 249-page manuscript, though Astra itself remains private.
