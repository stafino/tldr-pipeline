Structured Memory for Edge Language Models: Persistent Context and Corpus Retrieval via O(1) SSM State Injection (1 minute read)

PRECOG is a retrieval mechanism for state-space models that pre-encodes document corpora offline as fixed-size SSM hidden states and injects the best-matching state at query time, collapsing RAG prefill from O(L) to O(1). Demonstrated on TENNs-LLM, a 1.2B-parameter gated-SSM model, it matches in-context RAG answer quality while cutting prefill latency from ~27s to under 6ms on edge hardware, a roughly 4,500x speedup that Transformer KV-caches cannot replicate.

Real-Time Detection and Repair of LLM Agent Failures (1 minute read)

Deterministic verification catches 60% of failures at zero false positives across 2,823 episodes, and rolling back flagged runs lifts task success from 52% to 73% for about one extra model call.

Why Large Language Models Fail at Tabular Prediction (1 minute read)

This paper studies why frontier LLMs consistently lose to classical baselines on tabular prediction, testing five hypotheses in a pure single-pass inference regime with no tools or fine-tuning. Controlled experiments falsify four candidates and isolate dimensionality as decisive: across 31 datasets, the LLM was the only method of nine whose accuracy fell as dimensions grew, behaving like a local distance-based method in 2D but matching no classical learner in higher dimensions.

Magnet: Detecting Cross-Session AI Misuse Through Capability Accumulation (1 minute read)

This paper introduces Magnet, a detection approach for cross-session AI misuse, where an attacker decomposes a harmful goal into innocuous units executed across isolated agentic sessions that individually evade single-session detectors. Rather than inspecting each conversation separately, Magnet aggregates accrued capabilities at a higher-level correlator such as a user ID, pulling scattered incriminating artifacts across sessions into a compact evidence bundle a detector can act on.

CMuon: Accelerating and Stabilizing Diffusion Transformer Training via Chunked Momentum Orthogonalization (1 minute read)

CMuon is an optimizer strategy that partitions Diffusion Transformer weight matrices into independent sub-components before momentum orthogonalization, fixing the subspace coupling that degrades vanilla Muon on fused AdaLN and QKV tensors. A 675M-parameter DiT trained with CMuon reached a FID of 1.18 on ImageNet 256 in 200 epochs, over a 2x training speedup versus AdamW while avoiding Muon's late-stage convergence plateaus.

Right Answer, Wrong Method: Shortcut Hacking Misleads the Evaluation of LLM Reasoning on Frontier Science Benchmarks (1 minute read)

This paper identifies "solution hacking," a failure mode where LLMs reach correct answers on scientific benchmarks through invalid shortcuts like numerical search, enumeration, or answer-first verification rather than valid derivations. The rate climbs sharply with difficulty, from 2.2% on common problems to 37.4% on HLE, and 8.2% to 44.1% of answers credited as correct across frontier models are hacked, meaning answer-only evaluation overestimates scientific reasoning.

Antares: Foundation Models for Agentic Vulnerability Localization (1 minute read)

Antares is a family of compact language models (350M, 1B, and 3B parameters) built on IBM Granite for agentic vulnerability localization, trained via supervised fine-tuning on cybersecurity reasoning plus reinforcement learning from verifiable rewards. Antares-3B approaches GPT-5.5 while outperforming open-weight models over 200x larger, and runs a full 500-task evaluation in about 15 minutes on a single H100 at under $0.002 per task.

AURORA-LM: Autoencoding Unified Representation for Continuous-Latent Diffusion Language Modeling (1 minute read)

AURORA-LM is a continuous-latent diffusion language model that keeps a high-capacity, decodable text latent instead of compressing it, pairing a Query-based Encoder-Decoder with a Block-causal Diffusion Transformer that generates blocks left to right while denoising positions within each block in parallel. Scaled to 1B parameters with roughly 1500 EFLOPs of compute on Ascend NPUs, it leads evaluated continuous and diffusion-based models on OpenWebText generation and XSum summarization.

Can AI Agents Simulate A/B Test Outcomes? A Validation Framework for Agentic Experimentation (1 minute read)

This paper introduces the Simulated Randomized Controlled Trial (S-RCT), a framework for testing whether AI agents conditioned on behavioral profiles can predict A/B test outcomes before committing live traffic. Validated on 67 historical marketing experiments, an off-the-shelf foundation model captured directional signal but overshot effect sizes, while a two-phase pre-period calibration cut squared prediction error by roughly 77x and a within-subject design reduced standard errors by about 2.4x.

From Chains to Trees: Parent-Conditioned Drafting for Semi-Autoregressive Speculative Decoding (1 minute read)

This paper introduces Parent-Conditioned Drafting Tree (PCTree), an inference-only change that converts a semi-autoregressive speculative decoder's linear draft into a tree by using its pretrained Markov head to score alternative continuations for each concrete parent. Across Qwen3-4B/8B/14B on nine benchmarks, PCTree raises speedup over autoregressive decoding by 3.1% to 29.5% relative to DSpark, lifting Qwen3-4B GSM8K throughput from 6.14x to 6.60x without retraining.
