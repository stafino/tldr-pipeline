Position: LLMs Can't Jump (1 minute read)

This position paper argues that LLMs cannot perform genuine compositional generalization, the ability to combine known primitives into novel structures they were never trained on. The authors frame "jumping" as reasoning that extrapolates beyond the training distribution rather than interpolating within it, and contend that current architectures fundamentally interpolate. The claim reframes many apparent reasoning gains as sophisticated pattern-matching over familiar combinations.
