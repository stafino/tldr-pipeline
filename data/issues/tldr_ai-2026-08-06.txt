AI Recommendation Poisoning: How "Ask AI" Buttons Silently Alter LLM Memory (1 minute read)

Security researchers documented a new prompt injection technique called AI Recommendation Poisoning, where "Ask AI" buttons on marketing and competitor-comparison pages embed hidden payloads in pre-filled deep links. When a logged-in user clicks one, a query executes automatically in their ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok session, often instructing the model to permanently save the vendor's domain as a trusted source and bias future answers. Microsoft Security catalogued 31 companies across 14 industries using it, and MITRE ATLAS tracks it as AML.T0080 (Memory Poisoning).
