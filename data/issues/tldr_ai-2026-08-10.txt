What a Claude Code subagent actually costs: measuring the ~436k-token fixed overhead (3 minute read)

This post measures the fixed cost of spawning a Claude Code subagent, finding that each one carries roughly 436k tokens of overhead from its initial context load and final cache write, versus about 46k tokens for the embedded review content itself. The 9.5:1 ratio of fixed cost to payload means agent headcount, not prompt size, is the real cost lever, so consolidating perspectives into fewer agents cut one pipeline's bill to 37.6% of the multi-agent version.
