Don't be a meat proxy (1 minute read)

The author argues against acting as a "meat proxy," relaying AI output verbatim in Slack threads, pull request feedback, or code review instead of engaging directly. Pasting a raw Claude response adds no value, since the recipient could query the model themselves with better context control. The real contribution is reading, understanding, and validating the output, then responding in your own words as evidence that you did that work.

Over 2,500 Organizations Impacted by LiteLLM Supply Chain Attack (1 minute read)

CloudSEK reports that the LiteLLM supply chain attack exposed over 434,000 CI/CD pipelines and impacted more than 2,500 organizations, including Nvidia, AWS, Samsung, and the London Stock Exchange Group. LiteLLM was never targeted directly; its CI pipeline automatically installed a compromised version of Trivy, which pushed two malicious LiteLLM builds to PyPI that ran on every Python invocation and harvested cloud keys, tokens, and AI provider secrets during a 40-minute window.
