Compression is prediction (1 minute read)

This post argues that lossless compression and language modeling are fundamentally the same problem: both work by predicting the next piece of data and spending fewer bits on what they expect. It walks through compression basics, from run-length encoding to the transform, model, and entropy-coder stages of tools like gzip and Brotli, to show that a better predictor is a better compressor.
