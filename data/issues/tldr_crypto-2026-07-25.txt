Russia’s largest bank Sberbank plans crypto trading infrastructure by December (5 minute read)

Sberbank, Russia's largest bank, plans to launch crypto trading infrastructure and a digital depository by Dec. 1, moving ahead of a regulatory framework that takes effect Sept. 1 and requires licensed intermediaries by July 2027. Public exchange trading will be capped at assets clearing Bank of Russia liquidity thresholds, including a 5 trillion ruble ($64B) market cap, while crypto payments for goods and services inside Russia stay banned.

Robinhood Chain's real-world assets jump fivefold as tokenized stocks start trading in bigger size (5 minute read)

Tokenized real-world assets on Robinhood Chain jumped roughly fivefold to about $70M in under two weeks, as a dozen tokenized stocks led by GameStop, Nvidia, and SpaceX each cleared over $500,000 in daily volume. Total value locked tripled since mid-July to about $312M, though stablecoins and memecoins still account for most of the chain's activity.
