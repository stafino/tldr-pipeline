I rebuilt a viral "3,888% return" TradingView strategy — it landed almost exactly at coin-flip odds (2 minute read)

A developer tried to reproduce a viral TradingView strategy that claimed a 3,888% return, 62.8% win rate, and 2.1 profit factor on 1-hour BTCUSDT with 10x leverage, and found the two indicators driving its entries were never published anywhere obtainable. Once rebuilt with verifiable substitutes, the strategy landed at roughly coin-flip odds. The post is a useful reminder that a backtest built on unavailable indicators can't be independently checked before real capital is at risk.

How to Audit an Anchor Program: A Practical Checklist (2 minute read)

This guide walks through auditing a Solana Anchor program as a deliberate check of the small set of places where Anchor's automatic validation gets bypassed, rather than a line-by-line read. It maps each instruction's authority (Signer plus has_one or explicit constraints), then greps for the escape hatches where safety was turned off: AccountInfo and UncheckedAccount, /// CHECK: comments, init_if_needed, raw invoke_signed, and manual deserialization. It closes with a paste-ready checklist covering signer, has_one, seeds/bump, and owner constraints.
