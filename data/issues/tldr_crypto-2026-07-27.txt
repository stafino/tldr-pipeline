Lido Unveils Curated Module v2 in Ethereum Staking Overhaul (1 minute read)

Lido launched Curated Module v2, requiring its 34 professional node operators to post ETH-denominated bonds against the stake they manage for the first time, with a new penalty framework that slashes bonds to compensate stakers for validator slashing, diverted rewards, or downtime. The upgrade begins migrating over 265,000 validators holding 8M staked ETH onto Ethereum's high-balance design, cutting Lido's validator count by roughly a third and trimming attestation load by about 29% per epoch.

Lido begins consolidating $16 billion worth of staked ETH as Curated Module v2 rolls out (1 minute read)

Lido began consolidating more than 8 million ETH, worth about $16B, onto Ethereum's post-Pectra 0x02 validators as part of its Curated Module v2 rollout, the protocol's largest Core upgrade since V2. The move lifts the share of staked ETH on 0x02 validators to roughly 52% from 32%, cuts Ethereum's total validator count by about a third, and requires curated operators to post seizable ETH collateral against slashing.

Ondo launches new execution network, calling it ‘evolution’ of Ondo Chain (1 minute read)

CEO Ian De Bode framed it as the successor to Ondo Chain rather than a parallel deployment, and it now underpins the company's Ondo Perps exchange, with asset transfers settling on Ethereum and support for additional chains planned.

SparkKitty malware hidden in mobile apps scans photos for crypto wallet seed phrases (1 minute read)

Check Point detailed SparkKitty, a cross-platform malware family that scans photos on infected Android and iOS devices for cryptocurrency wallet seed phrases using optical character recognition, extracting recovery credentials without keylogging or clipboard monitoring. The malware spread through trojanized apps on Apple's App Store, Google Play, and third-party stores, including an Android app called SOEX that passed 10,000 downloads before removal.

Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong criticizes crypto startups that rebrand to AI (5 minute read)

Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong criticized crypto startups rebranding as AI companies, arguing in a Sunday X post that crypto is general-purpose infrastructure that underpins AI rather than competing with it. He framed Coinbase's push into what he calls Agentic Finance, built on the x402 protocol, Base, and USDC, as letting autonomous agents trade and settle payments in real time.
