Zcash activates Ironwood upgrade, launching new shielded pool after Orchard vulnerability (1 minute read)

Zcash activated its Ironwood network upgrade at block 3,428,143, launching a new formally verified shielded pool and restricting the old Orchard pool to withdrawals only after a bug in Orchard's zero-knowledge circuit could have allowed undetectable ZEC counterfeiting. About 40,207 ZEC have migrated into Ironwood so far, with roughly 3.6M still parked in Orchard, which once held nearly 88% of the chain's shielded supply.

1inch launches Aqua publicly, a shared liquidity layer for DeFi across 13 chains (1 minute read)

1inch launched Aqua publicly across 13 EVM chains, a shared, self-custodial liquidity layer that lets DeFi protocols draw from a common pool rather than fragmenting capital per venue. The rollout includes a 10M 1INCH incentive program to bootstrap liquidity providers, targeting the fragmentation that splits depth across isolated pools on each chain.

Hyperliquid is taking crypto perps deep into DeFi’s ‘money LEGO’ land (5 minute read)

Hyperliquid is turning its perps DEX into shared backend infrastructure, connecting its Ethereum-compatible HyperEVM to the homegrown HyperCore chain so wallets and exchanges can build on its liquidity rather than fragment it. Hundreds of developers, including MetaMask, Phantom, and VALR, now route through Hyperliquid's builder codes, generating roughly $90M in revenue to date.

Zcash seals $1.7 billion shielded pool as Ironwood upgrade activates (5 minute read)

Zcash activated its Ironwood (NU6.3) upgrade at block 3,428,143, sealing the Orchard shielded pool that holds about 3.66 million ZEC (roughly $1.7B) and opening a new pool that starts at zero, with a turnstile capping withdrawals at verifiable deposits. The move follows the disclosure of an Orchard proof-circuit bug that could mint counterfeit ZEC untraceably, and Ironwood adds quantum-resilient record-keeping and formally verified circuits.
