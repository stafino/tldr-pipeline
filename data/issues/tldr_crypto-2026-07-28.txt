Melee Launches Parimutuel Market Maker for Solana Prediction Markets (5 minute read)

Melee launched a parimutuel market maker for prediction markets on Solana, pooling all stakes on an outcome and paying winners proportionally from the losing side rather than quoting prices through an order book or constant-product curve. The design removes the need for dedicated liquidity providers, letting low-volume or long-tail markets settle without the thin books and slippage that typically plague on-chain prediction venues.

There Will Be Spam: Characterizing State-Invariant Transactions and Speculative MEV (1 minute read)

This paper introduces "state-invariant" transactions, ones whose inclusion or removal changes nothing beyond fees, and measures roughly 1.4B of them across Ethereum, Optimism, and Base. They make up just 2.6% of Ethereum transactions but 24% on Optimism and 37% on Base, with speculative MEV driving 57% and 68% of the L2 total respectively. The authors also tie 53% of non-reverted state-invariant transactions on Ethereum to address-poisoning campaigns, arguing that curbing them would cut resource use and phishing at once.

Why vaults are the wrong structure for fixed-rate lending (5 minute read)

The author argues that vaults are structurally wrong for fixed-rate lending because they hide who actually underwrites the debt. A fixed-rate loan carries specific duration and credit risk, but a vault depositor picks none of it, becoming counterparty to whatever loans the pool funds while still expecting instant withdrawals. The mismatch means duration risk and bad debt get socialized across everyone in the vault, rather than sitting with a real counterparty who accepted the terms and gets paid for them.

Strategies for quantum-enabled Bitcoin miners (1 minute read)

This paper extends a game-theoretic Bitcoin mining framework to model two competing miners running purpose-built quantum computers, each racing to find a valid block in an otherwise classical network. The authors compute payoff matrices for non-colluding aggressive quantum miners, including a novel Aggressive Quantum Mining Strategy that lets miners restart their search after a failed measurement. Even under optimal play, the quantum strategies have a negligible effect on Bitcoin's 51% attack threshold.

Zilliqa Ledger app vulnerability lets attackers recover signer's private keys (5 minute read)

Zilliqa disclosed a vulnerability in its Ledger app that generates ECDSA signatures with predictably weakened ephemeral nonces, letting attackers recover a signer's private key from public onchain data. Any wallet that signed at least five native Zilliqa transactions with a Ledger device is considered compromised. Zilliqa is coordinating a fixed app release with Ledger; users on EVM-compatible tooling were unaffected, and exchanges were asked to pause ZIL deposits and withdrawals.

Bitcoin Mempool Linearization (1 minute read)

This paper formulates the mempool linearization problem: computing a dependency-respecting transaction ordering that maximizes fee-rate efficiency while supporting fast updates as the mempool changes. The authors introduce spanning forest linearization (SFL), which operates directly on the transaction dependency graph and iteratively merges and splits chunks to reach a provably optimal ordering. On synthetic and real mempool data, SFL computes optimal linearizations faster than prior methods, and it has been incorporated into Bitcoin Core for transaction cluster linearization.

How onchain analysts demix Tornado Cash transactions (5 minute read)

This walkthrough breaks down how onchain analysts de-anonymize Tornado Cash withdrawals, using deposit-and-withdrawal timing correlation, reused relayers, gas-funding trails, and address-clustering heuristics to link mixed funds back to their source. The author illustrates each technique with real transaction traces, showing how the mixer's anonymity set collapses when users batch deposits, reuse wallets, or withdraw to previously linked addresses.

ZKP Security Tools and Verification: Coverage, Effectiveness, Adoption, and Challenges (1 minute read)

This paper surveys the security tooling landscape for zero-knowledge proofs, finding that most tools target Circom while newer DSLs and zkVMs remain undersupported. Across 70 real-world vulnerabilities, six tested tools caught 45.7% of bugs on isolated targets but only 19.6% on full codebases, leaving major vulnerability classes unaddressed. A survey of 48 practitioners shows development and auditing stay human-led, LLMs are widely used, and teams favor tools with clearer guarantees and lower integration cost.

Morpho Midnight Analysis (5 minute read)

Morpho launched Midnight, a fixed-term loan product that prices loans like bonds: lenders buy a credit unit at a discount that redeems at par at maturity, locking the rate rather than letting it reprice each block. The design targets institutional loan books already on Morpho, including Coinbase's $1.4B+ cbBTC-backed facility. Terms across 1, 3, and 6 months form a yield curve, though offer-based liquidity remains unproven at scale.
