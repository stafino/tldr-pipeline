NEAR Governance Votes to Scrap Developer Gas Rebate (1 minute read)

NEAR's House of Stake governance passed HSP-027 to scrap the developer gas rebate, redirecting the 30% of smart-contract gas fees that previously went to contract owners into the burn, so 100% of gas fees are now destroyed. The change, expected around August 2026 with nearcore v2.14, makes NEAR issuance more deflationary and marks the on-chain body's first test of authority over the token's core economic parameters.

Active Crypto VC Firms Fall to 150, Lowest Since 2020 (1 minute read)

Only 150 unique venture firms participated in crypto funding rounds in July, the lowest monthly count since November 2020 and down 87% from the May 2022 peak of 1,177, per CryptoRank. Deal capital hasn't dried up, with Dragonfly closing a $650M fourth fund in February, but the smaller funds, family offices, and angel syndicates that crowded 2021-2022 rounds have exited, concentrating deals among a shrinking circle of established firms.

10 European Banks Launch RL1 Blockchain Cooperative (1 minute read)

Ten European banks including ABN AMRO, DZ BANK, and Natixis CIB launched Regulated Layer One, a jointly owned permissioned blockchain for digital bond issuance, tokenized real-world assets, onchain collateral, and repo margining. Built on SWIAT infrastructure and structured as a Luxembourg cooperative with one-member-one-vote governance, RL1 has run in production for three years, settling over 50 transactions worth more than €700M.

Tether’s GENIUS-compliant USAT stablecoin launches on Celo, marking first expansion beyond Ethereum (1 minute read)

Tether launched its GENIUS Act-compliant USAT stablecoin on Celo, its first deployment beyond Ethereum since the token went live in January. USAT supports native mint and burn on Celo and can pay gas fees directly via the network's CIP-64 fee abstraction, which already makes Celo the top distribution network for Tether's USDT with 28% of cross-chain transfers.

Stablecoin firm Brale says new protocol can remove a major hurdle to scaling custom tokens (5 minute read)

Brale introduced ION Protocol, a testnet interoperability system that moves participating stablecoins across chains by burning tokens on one network and minting an equivalent amount on another, avoiding the pre-funded liquidity pools most bridges require. The design targets fragmentation in a market that now spans more than 350 tokens and over $300B in market cap, cutting the capital issuers must lock up per supported chain.

About $80 million ZEC crosses into Zcash's new Ironwood pool in the first day (5 minute read)

Zcash activated its Ironwood shielded pool, and about 176,000 ZEC, or roughly $81 million, migrated over on the first day, sealing the older Orchard pool where a counterfeiting flaw had gone undetected for four years. Orchard can no longer accept deposits and now holds around 3.51 million ZEC, with coins exiting only through a turnstile that caps withdrawals at the amount verifiably deposited.

Morpho Signs First Hong Kong Partnership With HashKey's HSK Chain (1 minute read)

Morpho will complete a full deployment on HashKey's HSK Chain and become the network's official onchain credit partner, marking the DeFi lending protocol's first anchor in Hong Kong's licensed virtual-asset regime. The two will build institutional and retail credit products with KYC and wallet screening layered on top, keeping Morpho's base contracts permissionless while HashKey, holder of an SFC Type 9 license, gates access at the edges.

Russia charges Telegram founder Pavel Durov with aiding terrorism, issues arrest warrant: report (1 minute read)

Russia's FSB charged Telegram founder Pavel Durov with aiding terrorist activity and placed him on an international wanted list, alleging the platform failed to remove channels used to coordinate attacks inside Russia, according to Interfax. The case follows more than 100 million rubles ($1.3 million) in fines this year and Durov's separate 2024 arrest in France, both of which he denies wrongdoing on.

Ethereum Foundation names pcaversaccio to board amid leadership changes (5 minute read)

The Ethereum Foundation appointed security researcher pcaversaccio, co-founder of the SEAL 911 emergency response initiative, to its board on a one-year voluntary term, bringing the group to four members alongside Aya Miyaguchi, Vitalik Buterin, and Patrick Storchenegger. The addition follows months of high-profile departures as the foundation narrows its role toward long-term stewardship rather than acting as the center of ecosystem development.

Crypto Long & Short: What this year's $972 million crypto hacks actually tell us about security (5 minute read)

Immunefi's Mitchell Amador argues that most of 2026's roughly $972M in stolen crypto has left through compromised keys, signers, and governance rather than smart contract bugs, citing a $20M BonkDAO treasury drain via a passed governance vote and a $30M Humanity Protocol loss from a stolen private key. He contends that passing a security audit was never equivalent to being safe, since the exploited rules and access paths sat outside the audited contract code.

The systemic-risk debate over perpetual futures is aimed at the wrong target (5 minute read)

A CoinDesk opinion piece argues that the systemic-risk critique of perpetual futures is misdirected, since a derivatives venue's risk comes from its design choices around leverage caps, margin, index construction, and default management, not from the no-expiry contract itself. The author, writing from exchange operator Bullish, adds that institutions treat perpetuals as a tool to hedge options delta rather than a replacement for dated futures.
