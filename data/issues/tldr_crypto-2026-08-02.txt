Circle secures New York trust charter as crypto regulatory push accelerates (5 minute read)

Circle secured a limited purpose trust charter from the New York Department of Financial Services, letting the USDC issuer offer fiduciary, custody, and asset-management services under New York banking law. The approval follows Circle's OCC national trust bank clearance earlier this month and joins peers Coinbase, Paxos, and BitGo, as USDC's market cap sits above $71.8B.

Coldcard Hardware Wallet Flaw Linked to $70 Million Bitcoin Theft in 41 Minutes (1 minute read)

An attacker drained 1,082.65 BTC worth about $70.2 million from 1,196 Bitcoin addresses in 41 minutes on July 30, a sweep Galaxy Research tied to a firmware flaw in Coinkite's Coldcard hardware wallet that routed seed generation through a weak software PRNG instead of the hardware RNG. Coinkite shipped emergency firmware on July 31, but it cannot repair an existing seed, so owners with exposed devices must generate a new seed and move their coins.

Metronome Discloses $15.7 Million Synth Shortfall, Blames Oracle Lag in Swap Module (1 minute read)

MetronomeDAO disclosed that roughly $15.7 million in msETH and msUSD are circulating with no collateral behind them, after trading bots spent months exploiting Chainlink oracle lag in the protocol's zero-slippage swap module. The shortfall equals 31% of all msETH and 16% of all msUSD, and any realized losses fall on liquidity providers in Curve and Aerodrome pools, with msETH down 25% in 24 hours.

Bank of Italy research suggests stablecoins aren't necessarily cheaper for remittances (5 minute read)

How bitcoin cold wallets lost $70 million in an attack that never touched the devices (5 minute read)

Galaxy Research found that weak seed generation, not device access, drained 1,082 BTC (about $70M) from 1,196 Coldcard wallets in a 41-minute window on July 30. An internal build flag told certain firmware to skip the dedicated hardware randomness generator, making supposedly unguessable seeds computationally enumerable and letting an attacker reconstruct private keys entirely offline. Owners cannot reliably tell whether their seeds came from vulnerable firmware, so security firms warn more wallets remain at risk.

Everyone has the perps convergence backwards (5 minute read)

In a CoinDesk opinion piece, Bitget CEO Gracy Chen argues the convergence narrative is inverted: rather than crypto maturing toward traditional finance, traditional assets are migrating onto perpetual futures, the market structure crypto invented. Real-world-asset perp volumes hit a record $211B in May 2026, roughly 16x their ~$12B level in Q4 2025, with equity perps up 121% month over month to $54B. The draw is continuous, globally accessible markets settled onchain, with no borrow desk to short and no contract to roll.

Hackers Poison Adform Script to Swap Crypto Wallet Addresses Across Customer Sites (1 minute read)

Attackers compromised a JavaScript file served by ad-tech firm Adform, injecting code that rewrote copied and typed Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Tron wallet addresses to redirect payments to attacker-controlled destinations. Because the poisoned trackpoint-async.js loads across many unrelated client sites, the incident is a supply-chain compromise; Adform detected it on July 27, removed the code, and is telling users to clear cached scripts and verify any address before sending funds.

Online ad firm Adform’s script compromised to steal cryptocurrency (1 minute read)

Adform, one of Europe's largest adtech firms, suffered a supply-chain attack that trojanized its trackpoint-async.js tracking script, monitoring visitor clipboards and swapping copied Bitcoin, Ethereum, and TRON wallet addresses for attacker-controlled ones across every site embedding the script. Adform removed the malicious code after researcher Kevin Beaumont flagged it, but no antivirus engine on VirusTotal detected the script, and affected users are advised to clear browser cookies.
