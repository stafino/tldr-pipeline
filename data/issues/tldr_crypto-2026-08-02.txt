When random.bytes() runs but doesn't work (1 minute read)

A guest post from Core-Lightning developer ddustin traces the recent Coldcard low-entropy bug through its firmware commit history, arguing that the flaw slipped in via two massive, unreviewed changesets. The commits that reworked random.bytes() on the STM32 carried messages of "runs" and "x" while touching over 2,500 lines combined, an almost nonexistent comment-to-code ratio for security-critical wallet code. The takeaway is that changes to key-generation paths demand smaller commits and stricter review.
