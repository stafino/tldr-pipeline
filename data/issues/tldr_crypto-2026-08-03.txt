BlackRock expands tokenized cash with new blockchain-based money market offerings (5 minute read)

BlackRock introduced two tokenized money market products, a share class of its Select Treasury Based Liquidity Fund (BSTBL) on Ethereum and a multichain Daily Reinvestment Stablecoin Reserve Vehicle (BRSRV), both designed to qualify as reserve assets for stablecoin issuers under the GENIUS Act. The firm already manages $60B in reserves for Circle and wants to become the industry's stablecoin reserve manager of choice.

Robinhood wins UK crypto registration before new regulatory regime kicks off (5 minute read)

Robinhood's UK arm was added to the FCA's register of cryptoasset companies as of July 31, clearing the regulator's anti-money-laundering requirements to offer crypto services to British users. The registration positions Robinhood ahead of the UK's comprehensive crypto framework taking effect in October 2027, whose authorization window runs from late September to February and favors firms already vetted under the existing regime alongside Ripple, Kraken, and BlackRock.

DEXs capture record 24% of spot crypto trading as CEX volumes sink (1 minute read)

DEX volume climbed to a record 24% of spot crypto trading, up from 17% a year ago, as centralized exchange volume slid to a 12-month low and firms including Coinbase and Gemini cut staff. Deeper aggregator liquidity and faster cross-chain swap routing have closed most of the execution gap that once made CEXs the default, pushing more spot flow onchain to venues like Uniswap and Aerodrome.

Yellow Card CEO on Funding, Stablecoin’s Global Foothold (1 minute read)

Yellow Card CEO Chris Maurice said the company differentiates from Stripe and Wise by building stablecoin infrastructure that provides direct liquidity and on/off ramps against local currencies across Africa, Latin America, and Asia. The setup lets users send and convert USDC locally without routing back through dollars first, targeting cross-border payments in markets underserved by traditional rails.

Blockchain Association counters National Sheriffs’ group claims as pivotal week for Clarity Act unfolds (1 minute read)

The Blockchain Association told Senate leaders that the National Sheriffs' Association misread the Clarity Act, arguing the bill imposes rigorous obligations on intermediaries and gives law enforcement tools to fight financial crime rather than exempting DeFi from AML rules. The dispute centers on Section 10604, a safe harbor for non-custodial developers, and lands as the Senate has only days before its August recess to move the bill.
