Ethereum researchers propose burning validator rewards to cap staking at 50% (1 minute read)

Ethereum researchers proposed EIP-8361, which would gradually burn a rising share of validator rewards as the network's staking ratio climbs toward 50%, making additional staking progressively less profitable. The mechanism aims to cap total staked ETH without a hard limit, preserving liquidity for DeFi and transaction fees while easing consensus-layer overhead from an ever-growing validator set.

New Ethereum Proposal Would Burn Validator Rewards to Kill the Incentive to Stake More (1 minute read)

Six authors including the Ethereum Foundation's Justin Drake published EIP-8361, "Tapered Issuance Burn," which destroys a rising fraction of validator rewards as more ETH is staked and drives net consensus-layer issuance to zero at a 50% staking ratio. Issuance currently supplies at least 93% of staking yield, so removing the yield floor lets the market, rather than the protocol, set the equilibrium staking level.

Isabelle/STARK: A Formalization of zk-STARK in Isabelle/HOL (1 minute read)

This paper presents Isabelle/STARK, a machine-checked formalization of a transparent zk-STARK proof protocol in Isabelle/HOL. The development includes an executable prover and verifier model, a finite probabilistic state monad with a weakest-precondition calculus, a zero-failure honest-completeness theorem, and a staged soundness theorem with an explicit probability bound. It targets readers with a formal-methods background, documenting the proof decomposition and the Isabelle source locations of the principal definitions and theorems.

From Viral to Void: Multi-Dimensional Behavioral and Contractual Analysis for Rug Pull Identification (1 minute read)

This paper introduces a multi-dimensional detection system for Ethereum meme-coin rug pulls, combining malicious contract design, on-chain transaction anomalies, liquidity manipulation, and social media signals into one feature set. The authors train an MLP with SMOTE oversampling and Focal Loss to handle class imbalance, reaching 0.927 accuracy and a 0.952 AUC-ROC on their test set. They ship a Flask-based tool for batch scoring and high-risk ranking, using the BOBU token collapse as a worked example.

COLDCARD wallet RNG flaw likely linked to $88 million Bitcoin theft (1 minute read)

Researchers linked a random number generator flaw in COLDCARD hardware wallet firmware to the theft of an estimated 1,367 BTC, worth roughly $88.6 million, from 4,585 addresses. Block's analysis found an integration error caused ngu.random to fall back to MicroPython's deterministic Yasmarang generator instead of the STM32 hardware RNG, making affected seeds predictable in a 41-minute automated sweep that preceded Coinkite's disclosure by 30 hours.
