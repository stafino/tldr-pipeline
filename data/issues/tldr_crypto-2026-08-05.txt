Aave And ether.fi Founders Lead Opposition To Ethereum's Staking Yield Burn (1 minute read)

Aave founder Stani Kulechov and ether.fi CEO Mike Silagadze are leading DeFi opposition to EIP-8363, a proposal to burn a rising share of Ethereum validator rewards that would cut net consensus yield from about 2.6% to 1.2% at today's staking level. Kulechov argues the mechanism accelerates the centralization it aims to prevent by filtering out economically motivated stakers, and core developers take up the debate Thursday against a 48-hour fork deadline.

Mastercard Extends Crypto Credential to Cross-Border Stablecoin Payments in Borderless.xyz Pilot (1 minute read)

Mastercard and Borderless.xyz began piloting Mastercard Crypto Credential for cross-border stablecoin payments, applying the card network's verification system so compliance signals travel with a provider once instead of being re-run at every counterparty. The live pilot launches with three operators—Infinia, Walapay, and Koywe—running a single-audit model across a stablecoin market that now totals about $308 billion in circulation.

Circle Names BlackRock, Visa, ICE and DTCC Among 11 Founding Arc Validators (1 minute read)

Circle named the 11 founding validators for Arc, its Layer 1 blockchain, including BlackRock, Visa, ICE, Mastercard, DTCC and Standard Chartered, ahead of a public mainnet launch set for Sept. 16. The permissioned validator set contradicts Circle's framing of Arc as "open and permissionless at its core," and the company has not said when participation opens beyond the named cohort.

New Ethereum proposal would cut issuance to zero if staked ETH reaches $112 billion (5 minute read)

Ethereum researchers proposed EIP-8361, which would burn a rising share of validator rewards as the staking ratio climbs, hitting a full burn and zero net issuance once roughly 60.25 million ETH (about half the supply) is staked. The design aims to cap staking by making additional stake less profitable, addressing concerns that rising yields concentrate ETH in large exchanges and staking providers, though it may miss the upcoming Hegotá upgrade.
