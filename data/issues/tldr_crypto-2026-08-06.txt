Towards Decentralized Searcher Competition in MEV Markets (1 minute read)

This paper studies MEV centralization from the searcher side, modeling how searchers differ in opportunity coverage and execution efficiency, and showing that standard first-price, winner-take-all auctions reward rank dominance over marginal contribution and concentrate rewards. The authors propose an entry-filtered, Shapley-capped auction that distributes rewards more broadly among admitted high-quality submissions, with Bayesian security constraints guarding against copied-code Sybil identities and validator-searcher collusion.

Por qué tu bot recibe 403 de Cloudflare (y cómo endurecer un cliente ccxt) (2 minute read)

This guide explains why ccxt trading bots hit intermittent 403 Forbidden errors from Cloudflare's WAF, which fronts many exchange REST APIs and challenges clients that "look like bots" even when the API key is valid. The author's ccxt-resilience library (Apache-2.0) mitigates it with two layers: harden(), which sets a browser User-Agent, Accept-Language, and generous timeout to draw fewer WAF challenges, and with_retry(), which retries only transient errors (403, 429, timeouts) with exponential backoff and jitter while re-raising auth and balance errors immediately.
