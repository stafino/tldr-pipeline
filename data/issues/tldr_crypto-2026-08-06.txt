Putin signs landmark crypto law allowing regulated retail trading in Russia (1 minute read)

Putin signed a law establishing Russia's first regulatory framework for crypto trading, licensing exchanges, custodians, and brokers while capping retail purchases at 300,000 rubles ($3,700) per intermediary annually and keeping the ban on using crypto for domestic payments. The framework takes effect September 1, 2026, and permits crypto in cross-border trade settlements, extending Russia's 2024 push to route around Western sanctions.

World Chain becomes first production L2 to ship streaming Block Access Lists via Flashblocks (1 minute read)

World Chain will become the first production L2 to stream full EIP-7928 Block Access Lists inside every flashblock, updating roughly every 200ms, with mainnet activation set for Aug. 17. The change lets validators verify independent transactions in parallel across CPU cores instead of re-executing sequentially, targeting up to 1 gigagas per second of throughput without heavier hardware.

Cloudflare Launches Stablecoin Wallets for AI Agents, Opens cloudflare.pay Handles (1 minute read)

Cloudflare launched Cloudflare Wallets, giving AI agents on its network a stablecoin balance and a human-readable cloudflare.pay handle to present when paying for APIs, content, and MCP tools. The product pairs Account Wallets for human owners with API-key-controlled Virtual Wallets governed by allowances, allow lists, and per-transaction caps, completing the buy side of the x402 payment stack Cloudflare began building with its July Monetization Gateway.

Towards Decentralized Searcher Competition in MEV Markets (1 minute read)

This paper studies MEV centralization from the searcher side, modeling how searchers differ in opportunity coverage and execution efficiency, and showing that standard first-price, winner-take-all auctions reward rank dominance over marginal contribution and concentrate rewards. The authors propose an entry-filtered, Shapley-capped auction that distributes rewards more broadly among admitted high-quality submissions, with Bayesian security constraints guarding against copied-code Sybil identities and validator-searcher collusion.
