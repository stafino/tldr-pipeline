ClickFix Attacks Deliver macOS Stealer That Can Drain Crypto Wallets (1 minute read)

ClickFix attacks are delivering a Go-based macOS stealer that tricks victims into pasting a command into Terminal, which fetches an architecture-matched Mach-O payload and exfiltrates browser passwords, iCloud Keychain data, and cached credentials. The notable addition is a DRAIN routine that checks wallet balances and siphons funds across Bitcoin, Litecoin, Dogecoin, Monero, Ethereum, and XRP, with logic to drain as little as 1% to stay under the radar. Huntress traced the payload and C2 infrastructure to sanctioned Russian host Aeza Group.

Bitcoin holders risk losing real BTC if they sell coins from BIP-110 fork, says developer (5 minute read)

A planned Bitcoin fork tied to the BIP-110 proposal could duplicate every holder's balance across two chains, and selling the new fork coins can trigger a replay attack because both chains initially accept identical signed transactions. A transaction broadcast to sell the fork version can be rebroadcast on the main chain, moving the seller's real BTC to the buyer. Developer Kevin Loaec warns that with no replay protection until at least early September, leaving coins unmoved is the only safe option for non-experts.
