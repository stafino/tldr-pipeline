Bitcoin hits block 961,632 as the controversial BIP-110 soft fork attempt begins (5 minute read)

Bitcoin reached block 961,632 on Saturday, triggering the mandatory signaling period for BIP-110, a soft fork that would temporarily curb non-financial data embedded on the network, with miner support seldom exceeding 2.5% against the 55% threshold required. Backers are now pushing it as a user-activated soft fork that relies on node operators rejecting non-signaling blocks, raising the prospect of a minority chain splitting from the hashpower-dominant mainnet.
