Bitcoin’s BIP-110 supporters split onto minority chain as main network pulls ahead (1 minute read)

Bitcoin's chain split at block 961,632 on Saturday when nodes enforcing BIP-110, a one-year rule restricting non-financial data like Ordinals inscriptions, began rejecting blocks that didn't signal support for the proposal. Only 2.53% of blocks signaled support over the prior two weeks, and the minority fork fell seven blocks behind the main chain within hours as it failed to attract meaningful mining power.

Solana lending giant Jupiter now lets the same dollar earn twice (5 minute read)

Jupiter released Lend v2 on Monday, letting deposits and borrowed positions on Solana double as trading liquidity so the same capital earns lending interest plus a share of swap fees. Optional Smart Collateral and Smart Debt features auto-pair assets into correlated pools, but yields depend on Jupiter's router sending enough swap flow, and collateral providers bear the loss if a stablecoin in the pair depegs.
