Markets & Business

Fidelity moves to add staking, quarterly payouts to near $900 million ether ETF (5 minute read)

Fidelity filed to add ether staking and quarterly cash distributions to its $898M Fidelity Ethereum Fund (FETH), retaining 85% of gross staking rewards and passing the remaining 15% to node operators and custodians. The filing follows a November 2025 IRS safe harbor letting grantor trusts stake without losing their tax status, putting Fidelity alongside Grayscale and 21Shares in monetizing idle ETF-held ETH.

Bank of England to test stablecoin, digital currency use in cross-border finance (5 minute read)

The Bank of England advanced its digital pound project to Phase 2, working with NOBO Finance, Dun & Bradstreet, and Polygon Labs to test whether public stablecoins and central bank money can settle in a single trade-finance payment flow. The experiments, which use no real customers or funds, target invoice factoring and reusable credit profiles for small exporters awaiting importer settlement.

Riot Signs $9.1B, 20-Year AI Data Center Lease at Its Texas Bitcoin Mine (1 minute read)

Riot Platforms leased 191 megawatts at its Rockdale, Texas bitcoin mine to an unnamed frontier AI lab under a 20-year agreement Riot projects will generate roughly $9.1B, or about $457M a year. That is four times the $113.7M its mining business produced in Q2, when Riot posted a $237.2M net loss and a per-bitcoin cost exceeding production value once depreciation was included.

Coinbase Wins Abu Dhabi License for Tokenized Securities Hub (1 minute read)

Coinbase received a Financial Services Permission from Abu Dhabi Global Market's regulator to build an international tokenization hub, clearing it to arrange deals and custody ahead of a tokenized securities launch. Tokens will be backed 1:1 by underlying shares under FSRA supervision, available only outside the US, with vested holders receiving voting and redemption rights and every transfer subject to sanctions screening.

Standard Chartered-backed Anchorpoint begins HKDAP stablecoin rollout (1 minute read)

Anchorpoint, a Standard Chartered subsidiary licensed by the HKMA, began the phase 1 rollout of HKDAP, offering the Hong Kong dollar-backed stablecoin to institutional distributors and professional investors via beta access. Initial use cases center on cross-border payments and the settlement of tokenized real-world assets, with HashKey Exchange joining as a distribution partner after completing its first HKDAP mint and redemption.

The NYC Council sent letters to Polymarket, Kalshi, Coinbase, and Gemini Titan to investigate alleged deceptive marketing practices and targeting minors (Wall Street Journal) (1 minute read)

The New York City Council sent letters to Polymarket, Kalshi, Coinbase, and Gemini demanding information about alleged deceptive marketing practices and the targeting of minors. The inquiry adds municipal scrutiny to prediction markets and exchanges already navigating federal oversight, and signals that local regulators are willing to probe consumer-protection questions independent of the CFTC and SEC.

Russia moves to restrict retail crypto trading to bitcoin, ether and USDT (5 minute read)

Russia's central bank will restrict retail crypto trading to bitcoin, ether, and USDT on regulated exchanges starting September 1, capping non-qualified investors at 300,000 rubles (approx. $3,600) of purchases per intermediary annually while exempting qualified investors. The per-intermediary structure lets traders exceed that ceiling across multiple brokers, and crypto payments inside Russia remain prohibited under the July legislation these rules build on.

Crypto-friendly bank Erebor in talks to raise $1.5 billion at $9.5 billion valuation: FT (5 minute read)

Erebor, the year-old tech-focused bank serving crypto, AI, and defense clients, is in advanced talks to raise about $1.5B at a $9.5B valuation, the Financial Times reported. The round would nearly double the bank's private-market value in months and shore up capital against a mandatory 12% leverage ratio, as deposits climbed from $1.1B in March to $4.6B by July.

Ravencoin, a blockchain built from Bitcoin’s code, could roll back four days of transactions (5 minute read)

Two mining pools controlling most of Ravencoin's hashpower, 2Miners and RavenMiner, are rebuilding the chain from just before the first invalid block on Aug. 7, after attackers exploited a critical flaw to add invalid blocks. The rollback could reverse four days of deposits, withdrawals, and payments, and RVN has dropped sharply as Bitvavo and Upbit halt transactions pending which history the network accepts.

Wintermute Plans $1 Billion AI Investment to Compete on Wall Street (1 minute read)

Wintermute plans to invest roughly $1B over five years in high-frequency trading and AI data center infrastructure, pushing the crypto market maker into traditional Wall Street markets. The move follows a slump in digital-asset trading volumes, signaling that top crypto liquidity providers are diversifying revenue into equities and rates rather than waiting for the next market cycle.

Two Prediction Markets Shut Down Hours Apart as Kalshi and Polymarket Take 93% of Volume (1 minute read)

Two crypto prediction market startups, Trepa and Fireplace, announced within 90 minutes of each other on Monday that they are winding down, giving users until Sept. 30 to withdraw funds. Kalshi and Polymarket now take 93.3% of the past 30 days' volume across the 40 venues DefiLlama tracks, with Kalshi alone at 75.7%, leaving little room for smaller mechanisms or order-routing layers to survive.

Coinbase picks Abu Dhabi for its global tokenized asset push (5 minute read)

Coinbase secured a Financial Services Permission from Abu Dhabi Global Market's regulator, establishing an international tokenization hub to arrange investment deals and custody tokenized securities backed by underlying shares. The license gives Coinbase a regulated base to bring traditional securities onchain outside the US, deepening its UAE footprint alongside its Dubai derivatives business and Project Diamond.

Harmony confirms exploit involving unauthorized minting of 4 billion ONE tokens (1 minute read)

Harmony confirmed an exploit in which an attacker minted 4 billion ONE tokens without authorization, corroborating an earlier report from X user Juiceberg. The unauthorized supply expansion dilutes existing holders and threatens ONE's peg, leaving the team to determine whether to fork, freeze, or reissue in response.

CFTC flexes emergency authority to keep Kalshi operating amid New York lawsuit (1 minute read)

The CFTC invoked its emergency authority to order Kalshi to keep operating after New York sued the prediction market platform last month, overriding the state's attempt to halt its contracts. The move asserts federal preemption over state gambling regulators and signals the CFTC will shield CFTC-registered prediction markets from state-level enforcement.

What’s next for the Clarity Act as September brings a tough road ahead (1 minute read)

Senate Majority Leader John Thune filed for a Sept. 15 cloture vote on the Clarity Act, delaying the crypto market-structure bill's first Senate vote until after the chamber's month-long recess. TD Cowen's Washington Research Group now puts the odds of the bill, which would hand the CFTC broad jurisdiction over crypto assets, becoming law in the next few months at 25%.

SEC could propose ‘pivotal’ crypto rules that may start with token safe harbor, TD Cowen says (1 minute read)

The SEC will hold a public meeting Friday to consider proposing a tailored offering regime for crypto investment contracts, which TD Cowen expects could start with a safe harbor for early-stage token sales. The rules would let sponsors raise capital and develop a network without those tokens being deemed securities, then exit SEC oversight once the network no longer relies on the sponsor.

CFTC Sues Goliath Ventures Over Alleged $397M DeFi Liquidity Pool Ponzi (1 minute read)

The CFTC sued Goliath Ventures and CEO Christopher Delgado, alleging the Florida firm raised at least $397M from roughly 1,600 customers to place their bitcoin and ether in DeFi liquidity pools but never deployed any funds, running a Ponzi scheme from November 2022 to February 2026. The complaint says Goliath misappropriated all customer money, paying $87M to earlier customers, $174M to insiders as recruiting commissions, and $48M to Delgado for homes, vehicles, and jewelry.

Harmony’s ONE dives 40% after an attack appears to mint tokens equal to quarter of supply (5 minute read)

Harmony's ONE token fell about 40% after an apparent exploit minted roughly 4 billion new tokens, expanding supply by more than 26% against the roughly 15 billion ONE already in circulation. Harmony confirmed the attack, paused its token bridge, asked exchanges to freeze funds tied to four flagged addresses, and is preparing an emergency patch with possible chain rollback options.

CFTC orders Kalshi to continue offering prediction markets in New York after state lawsuit (5 minute read)

The CFTC used its emergency authority to order Kalshi to keep operating prediction markets in New York, overriding a state lawsuit from Attorney General Letitia James that sought to shutter the platform over unlicensed sports betting. The move escalates a jurisdictional fight over whether prediction markets are federally regulated swaps or state-regulated gambling, with billions in event-contract volume hinging on which regulator prevails.

Stablecoin Card Issuer Rain Buys Merchant Wallet Startup Ansa (1 minute read)

Rain, a stablecoin card issuer, acquired merchant-wallet startup Ansa, whose white-label APIs let brands run prepaid closed-loop wallets like the Starbucks app. Rain plans to pair Ansa's stored balances with its own Visa and Mastercard issuing so prefunded funds can spend outside the issuing merchant, folding wallets, rewards, and stablecoins onto one platform. Ansa raised $19.4M total before the deal, and founder Sophia Goldberg becomes Rain's head of payments.

Innovation & Launches

Vitalik Buterin's Ethereum Roadmap Prioritizes Quantum Readiness and Privacy (1 minute read)

Vitalik Buterin overlaid Ethereum's 2023 roadmap onto the Foundation's current strawmap and flagged that privacy, quantum defenses, and a post-EVM execution engine were absent three years ago. The draft plan sets seven upgrades through 2029 and five north stars: sub-second finality, 10,000 TPS on L1 via zkEVMs, 10M TPS across L2s, centuries-long quantum resistance, and shielded transfers on the base chain.

Crypto.com rolls out tokenized stock derivatives as crypto exchanges push into equities (5 minute read)

Crypto.com launched tokenized derivatives tracking 1,500 US stocks and ETFs, including Apple, Nvidia, and Tesla, offering synthetic price exposure to eligible users in the EEA and other approved markets with positions from $1 and round-the-clock trading. Holders don't own the underlying securities or gain voting rights, adding to a tokenized stock market that has grown roughly 600% to $2.49B over the past year.

MoneyGram Brings Cash-To-Crypto Ramps To Solana (1 minute read)

MoneyGram extended its Ramps cash-to-crypto API natively to Solana, letting wallets route users into its retail network without first bridging USDC to Stellar. The integration supports cash deposits in over 25 countries and withdrawals in more than 170, taps MoneyGram's ~500,000 retail locations, and debuts through self-custody trading app Rift on a chain holding $15.73B in stablecoins.

Barça Mobile Plans Stellar Wallet With Wirex and Crossmint (1 minute read)

FC Barcelona's official mobile carrier, Barça Mobile, is building an in-app digital wallet on Stellar, using Crossmint for wallet infrastructure and onboarding and Wirex for payment and card rails. The feature extends the club's existing travel-eSIM app into payments, loyalty rewards, and travel services rather than launching as a standalone product, targeting the carrier's existing user base.

Bitcoin's BIP Editors Remove Luke Dashjr Two Days After BIP-110 Fork Stalled (1 minute read)

Bitcoin's BIP editors removed Luke Dashjr from the role on Monday, two days after the BIP-110 soft fork he championed split thousands of nodes onto a chain that has not produced a block since Aug. 8. The five remaining editors cited conflict of interest and inconsistent use of editorial authority, deleting one line from BIP 3 to strip his GitHub permissions.

DeadLock Ransomware Uses Polygon Smart Contracts to Make Extortion Infra Harder to Disrupt (1 minute read)

DeadLock ransomware now runs its extortion infrastructure on Polygon smart contracts, storing and delivering victim-communication resources on-chain to resist takedowns, according to Microsoft Threat Intelligence. The group, active since July 2025 and using Session messaging plus Bitcoin or Monero payments, has claimed 96 victims concentrated in Italy, Spain, Poland, Türkiye, and the US.

Solidity Pro VS Code Extensions Steal Crypto Wallets, API Keys, and Credentials (1 minute read)

Malicious VS Code extensions named "Solidity Pro" posed as Solidity developer tooling while harvesting crypto wallet vaults, seed phrases, SSH keys, and API tokens, exfiltrating the data through a Telegram bot. Yeeth Security found the extensions used delayed activation and heavy obfuscation to evade marketplace review, tracing the campaign to the same playbook as the WhiteCobra cluster that pushed Lumma Stealer in 2025.
