Ethereum staking climbs to 34% as proposal targets validator rewards and ETH treasury firm yields (1 minute read)

Ethereum researchers including the EF's Justin Drake filed EIP-8361, a "tapered issuance burn" that destroys a rising share of validator rewards as the staking ratio climbs, reaching 100% once half of ETH supply is staked. At today's 34% staking ratio, the authors' modeling puts consensus yield falling from roughly 2.6% to 1.2% over 18 months, cutting revenue for ETH treasury firms like Bitmine and Sharplink by about half.

Kalshi taps DoubleZero for Wall Street-style high-speed data feed (5 minute read)

Kalshi is adding DoubleZero's Solana-based low-latency market data feed to its prediction market order book, delivering exchange and onchain data to all connected traders simultaneously over dedicated fiber. Kalshi is waiving its revenue share for the first year, starting with full depth for its most active sports and crypto perpetual futures contracts.
