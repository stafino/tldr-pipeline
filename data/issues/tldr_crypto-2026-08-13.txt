The CFTC sued Goliath Ventures and CEO Christopher Delgado, alleging the Florida firm raised at least $397M from roughly 1,600 customers to place their bitcoin and ether in DeFi liquidity pools but never deployed any funds, running a Ponzi scheme from November 2022 to February 2026. Blockchain Association backs Custodia’s Supreme Court bid over Fed master account access (1 minute read)

The Blockchain Association filed an amicus brief backing Custodia Bank's Supreme Court petition, which challenges whether regional Fed banks can deny master account access to eligible state-chartered institutions. Custodia, the Wyoming crypto bank founded by Caitlin Long, has lost at the district court, the 10th Circuit, and an en banc rehearing since first applying in 2020, making a master account the gating infrastructure for banks serving digital asset clients.

Stablecoin Card Issuer Rain Buys Merchant Wallet Startup Ansa (1 minute read)
