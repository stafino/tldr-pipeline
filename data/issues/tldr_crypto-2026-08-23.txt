Russia moves to restrict retail crypto trading to bitcoin, ether and USDT (5 minute read)

Russia's central bank will restrict retail crypto trading to bitcoin, ether, and USDT on regulated exchanges starting September 1, capping non-qualified investors at 300,000 rubles (approx. $3,600) of purchases per intermediary annually while exempting qualified investors. The per-intermediary structure lets traders exceed that ceiling across multiple brokers, and crypto payments inside Russia remain prohibited under the July legislation these rules build on.
