Design is compromise (1 minute read)

Steph Ango argues that "compromise" is not a dirty word but the essence of design: every approach chooses one strength at the cost of another, so the products worth admiring are the opinionated ones that state plainly what they are bad at. The reframe is a useful corrective to "no compromises" marketing, and a reminder that trying to be good at everything is itself the choice to be exceptional at nothing.
