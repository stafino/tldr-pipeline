How Nomad reimagined Stoiximan, the homegrown brand that became Greece's biggest name in betting (1 minute read)

Nomad rebranded Stoiximan, Greece's largest iGaming platform, around a bespoke wordmark with a win-celebrating figure hidden inside the "x" and an interlocking "S" that anchors the motion system. The work spans a flexible "Portal" graphic system plus Stoiximan Sans, a custom Greek-and-Latin typeface built with Studio Drama, designed to hold up from phone screens to floodlit stadium boards.

FSB relaunches 1986 Dieter Rams door handle with "effortless grip" (1 minute read)

FSB relaunched the FSB 1138 door handle, Dieter Rams' 1986 design of a circular rose, index-finger recess, and rounded lever, rebuilt in all-aluminium construction after the original's thermoplastic parts led to its 2005 discontinuation. The metal version drops the failure-prone plastic component and will develop a patina over time, preserving the "less but better" form while fixing the durability problem that killed the original.

Athens apartment features "fluid layout" that adapts to houseguests (1 minute read)

Greek architect Georgios Apostolopoulos reworked a minimalist Athens apartment in the Kifisia neighbourhood with sliding walls, hidden doors, and a fold-down bed that let the entire floor plan reconfigure for overnight guests. The project treats spatial flexibility as the core layout logic rather than a bolt-on feature, a useful reference for anyone designing small residential interiors where one footprint has to serve multiple modes.

Unfold Projects returns mid-century California residence to a "light-filled, organic oasis" (1 minute read)

Unfold Projects renovated a 1970s modernist home in Encinitas, California, originally designed by Shanna Kellogg with structural work from her father Kendrick Bangs Kellogg, converting it into a base for an artist residency program. The studio preserved the property's signature arched roofs while reworking the interiors around natural light and organic materials, positioning the restoration as a working space rather than a private residence.

Koto helps restaurant-discovery app Franki make time dance (1 minute read)

Koto gave Franki, the merchant-funded restaurant-discovery app, a warm yellow-soaked identity built on the line "make time dance," positioning it against Google, Tripadvisor, Yelp, and a wave of video-first discovery rivals. The concept treats time as the currency of dining out, translating into an energetic, motion-led system meant to sell an app-only experience and keep users posting recommendations after download.

Lenka Milerová Architektka transforms stone pigsty in Czech Republic into holiday home (1 minute read)

Lenka Milerová Architektka converted a former stone pigsty in Panoší Újezd, Czech Republic into a summer house, preserving the rough stone and brick walls while adding exposed concrete, steel-framed openings, and a boardmarked-concrete bathroom carved into the slope. A single concrete bench wraps the one-room interior to serve as step, wood storage, sofa, and bathtub surround, keeping the intervention deliberately raw rather than polished.
