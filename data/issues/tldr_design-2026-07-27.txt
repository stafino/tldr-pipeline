Accessibility paywall on Meta Glasses ‘paused’ after condemnation [U] (1 minute read)

Meta paused plans to paywall Conversation Focus, the accessibility feature on Ray-Ban Meta Glasses that amplifies a speaker's voice in noisy settings, after backlash over its proposed three-hour monthly cap and $19.99 Meta One Premium upsell. The reversal followed criticism that the feature runs entirely on-device, giving no server-cost justification for charging existing owners who bought the hardware partly for that capability.

The consciousness mirage in AI design (1 minute read)

The author argues that anthropomorphizing AI is not user irrationality but an evolved response to treating language as evidence of another mind, and proposes two frameworks, the Mind Attribution Ladder and the Empathy Trough, to map how that perception forms and recalibrates. The practical payoff is treating mind attribution as a design dial, building conversational trust without inflating what users believe the system actually understands.
