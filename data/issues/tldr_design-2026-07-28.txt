Why stadium design is getting more outlandish (1 minute read)

Stadium design is shifting toward icon-first architecture as clubs treat their venues as brand statements, exemplified by Heatherwick Studio's Birmingham City design that wraps the stands in 12 giant chimneys. The trend reflects a commercial reality where teams earn as much from merchandise as TV rights, pushing owners like Real Madrid, whose €1B Bernabéu overhaul was led by GMP Architekten, to commission landmark structures over conventional bowls.

Thinking Outside The Box: Digital Design In The AI Era (1 minute read)

MacPaw's design team argues that conversational textboxes have made AI assistants feel transactional, and details how they rethought the format around a character-like presence to make AI feel more personal without displacing the human at the center. The piece doubles as a case study in designing anthropomorphic AI and a defense of empathy and human authorship as differentiators in an era of faceless chatbots.

Ten UNESCO-listed Soviet modernist buildings in Tashkent (1 minute read)

UNESCO added ten of Tashkent's 1960s–80s Soviet modernist buildings to its World Heritage list, protecting works like the Hotel Uzbekistan and the Chorsu Bazaar dome that had faced demolition pressure. The listing followed the multi-year Tashkent Modernism XX/XXI research and preservation project, giving formal legal cover to a body of brutalist and regional-modernist work long dismissed as expendable.
