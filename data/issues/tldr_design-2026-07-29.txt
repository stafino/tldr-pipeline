Combining Computational Design with Wicker (1 minute read)

Priyanka Narula and her Hyderabad-based Prelab Design Studio built Wicker Story, a system that translates parametric, algorithmically complex geometries into traditional Indian wicker weaving without industrial fabrication infrastructure. The essay argues that pairing computational design with local craft yields zero-waste, customizable forms while sustaining regional labor and material knowledge, positioning craft as a production method rather than a decorative afterthought.
