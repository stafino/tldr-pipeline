New York loft renovation by Studio Vural comprises "pods in a box" (1 minute read)

Studio Vural renovated a West Village loft inside a 1920s former herring canning factory, organizing the space as freestanding "pods in a box" with tonal green accents drawn from the site's Vita Herring heritage. The scheme keeps the raw industrial shell legible while slotting self-contained functional volumes inside it, a strategy that reads as a cleaner alternative to conventional partition walls in open-plan lofts.
