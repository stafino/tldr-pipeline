Catalan farmhouse designed as "a passage through the land" (1 minute read)

NUA Arquitectures completed Casa Juià, a 283-square-metre farmhouse in the Catalan village of Juià that reinterprets the region's traditional farmhouse as three parallel wings wrapped in porches and galleries. The layout draws on local craftsmanship and the Gavarres foothills setting, framing the house as a series of shaded thresholds between built space and land rather than a single sealed volume.

Martin Hopp Architect puts generosity over spectacle at mass-timber school in New York (1 minute read)

Martin Hopp Architect completed Evergreen Charter School, an 89,000-square-foot K-12 building in Hempstead, New York, built on a mass-timber structure that prioritizes healthy materials and daylit spaces on a tight budget. The Long Island project, opened in 2025, treats exposed wood and generous common areas as the design payoff rather than a signature facade, a restraint-driven approach to institutional work.

São Paulo skyscrapers by Königsberger Vannucchi linked by glass skybridge (1 minute read)

Königsberger Vannucchi Arquitetos Associados connected the Almagah 227 residential and office towers in São Paulo with a horizontal glass skybridge, part of the larger Eixo Platina mixed-use development by Porte Engenharia e Urbanismo. The bridge is meant to preserve each tower's individual reading while tying them into a single composition, a legibility-versus-unity balance that governs how paired high-rises get read from the street.

Get Ready For the Powerful CSS border-shape Property! (1 minute read)

CSS is getting a new border-shape property that shapes an entire element while letting decorative properties like border, box-shadow, and outline follow that shape rather than the box. Unlike clip-path, which clips everything including borders, border-shape accepts the same values including the new shape() function, so it fills the long-standing gap of adding proper borders to CSS shapes.
