How Stupid Never Dies built one of gaming's freshest art styles by redefining punk from scratch (1 minute read)

GPTRACK50's debut game Stupid Never Dies built its visual style by defining "zombie" and "punk" from scratch, listing every element of punk fashion, attitude, and iconography rather than pulling from Pinterest boards or reference books. The result trades survival-horror gloom for loud, high-saturation chaos, mixing comic-book exaggeration, skate culture, graffiti, and grotesque-but-lovable monsters into one of gaming's freshest art directions.
