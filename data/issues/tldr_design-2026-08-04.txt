Did Coca-Cola need to rebrand? Design experts weigh in on the new look (1 minute read)

How DNCO turned Vermont from a place people admire into one they actually want to visit (1 minute read)

DNCO developed State of Inspiration, Vermont's first dedicated tourism brand, to close a gap the state's research laid bare: 79% of Americans find Vermont appealing but only 15% intend to visit. Rather than sell the usual escape-and-slow-down positioning, the studio reframed the state around finding, gaining, and learning, betting that a reason to come now matters more than being liked.

SBP and Snøhetta cantilever bridge between rock faces in Upstate New York (1 minute read)

Schlaich Bergermann Partner and Snøhetta replaced a decaying span in Watkins Glen State Park with the Sentry Bridge, a steel half-arch mounted on one gorge wall that cantilevers across to the opposite rock face. The single-anchor structure avoids sinking foundations into the fragile canyon, letting the deck read as a thin line stretched between the cliffs rather than a truss resting on them.

Glazed pavilion with "distinctly modernist character" added to Portland Art Museum (1 minute read)

Hennebery Eddy Architects and Vinci Hamp Architects added a glass-walled pavilion between two historic buildings at the Portland Art Museum, unifying the downtown campus across two urban blocks and reworking visitor circulation. The modernist insertion connects previously separate structures under one entry sequence, a restraint-versus-contrast move that lets the new glazed volume read as distinct without competing with the older facades it joins.

Design.md: the one standard file carries your visual identity, for humans and agents (3 minute read)

Gap Decorations Are Now Available, Here’s What’s New (1 minute read)

CSS gap decorations are now available in Chrome and Edge starting with version 149, extending the column-rule property to grid and flexbox layouts and adding a matching row-rule property for two-axis control. Led by Microsoft's Edge team, the feature replaces the border and pseudo-element hacks designers previously relied on to style layout gaps, and works as a progressive enhancement that degrades cleanly on unsupported browsers.

Autodesk tackles AI filmmaking's biggest problem by offering 3D control (1 minute read)

Autodesk expanded Flow Studio with a 3D Editor + Canvas workflow that lets creators block scenes, direct performances, and set camera movement and timing in a 3D workspace before generating final visuals in a node-based 2D editor. The split separates direction from rendering, letting teams stay in the faster 2D flow for simple shots or move into 3D for spatial control over composition, cameras, and pacing on long-form work.
