Red scaffolding surrounds temporary pavilion in India (1 minute read)

Kanan Modi Associates built The Reclaimed Pavilion, a 1,500-square-metre temporary events space in Hyderabad, from a grid of red scaffolding and materials salvaged from local construction sites. Commissioned by developer Jayabheri Group for a fast-growing neighbourhood, the project reframes reuse as a visible design language rather than a hidden sustainability credential.

MAD scatters boulder-like galleries across green-roofed museum in Shenzhen (1 minute read)

MAD completed Shenzhen Bay Culture Square in the city's Nanshan District, a design museum of stone-like gallery volumes scattered across an undulating green roof that doubles as coastal public space. The scheme frames the museum as both a cultural hub and a walkable landscape, extending Houhai's waterfront rather than sealing the galleries off behind a single monumental facade.

Ómós guesthouse designed to "feel like nothing else in Ireland" (1 minute read)

Asca Studio and Relief Gallery designed the interiors of Ómós, a 16-room guesthouse near Abbeyleix that Ryan W Kennihan Architects converted from a dilapidated Victorian ruin into a space layered with tactile, locally sourced materials. The project leans on texture and craft rather than a signature aesthetic, aiming for a hospitality interior that reads as specific to its Irish setting.
