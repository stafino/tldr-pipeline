Stan Hema rebrands Stadtmuseum Berlin as the Berlin Museum, with a wordmark built from the city itself (1 minute read)

Stan Hema rebranded Berlin's Stadtmuseum as the Berlin Museum, consolidating locations like the Märkisches Museum and Nikolaikirche under one name and a flexible wordmark assembled from typography found across the city, from U-Bahn signage to the local currywurst stand. Rather than a fixed logo, the identity uses a system that shifts and combines these street-sourced typefaces, a direct response to public feedback that the institution read as a dusty, hard-to-place history museum.
