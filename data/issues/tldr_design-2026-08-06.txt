Red scaffolding surrounds temporary pavilion in India (1 minute read)

Kanan Modi Associates built The Reclaimed Pavilion, a 1,500-square-metre temporary events space in Hyderabad, from a grid of red scaffolding and materials salvaged from local construction sites. Commissioned by developer Jayabheri Group for a fast-growing neighbourhood, the project reframes reuse as a visible design language rather than a hidden sustainability credential.

MAD scatters boulder-like galleries across green-roofed museum in Shenzhen (1 minute read)

MAD completed Shenzhen Bay Culture Square in the city's Nanshan District, a design museum of stone-like gallery volumes scattered across an undulating green roof that doubles as coastal public space. The scheme frames the museum as both a cultural hub and a walkable landscape, extending Houhai's waterfront rather than sealing the galleries off behind a single monumental facade.

Ómós guesthouse designed to "feel like nothing else in Ireland" (1 minute read)

Asca Studio and Relief Gallery designed the interiors of Ómós, a 16-room guesthouse near Abbeyleix that Ryan W Kennihan Architects converted from a dilapidated Victorian ruin into a space layered with tactile, locally sourced materials. The project leans on texture and craft rather than a signature aesthetic, aiming for a hospitality interior that reads as specific to its Irish setting.

Adobe brings 70 tools across its creative and productivity suites to ChatGPT (1 minute read)

Adobe brought roughly 70 tools from its creative and productivity suites, including Photoshop and Express actions, into ChatGPT, letting users invoke editing and design workflows from a single prompt. The move puts Adobe's capabilities inside OpenAI's chat interface rather than its own apps, testing whether designers will accept conversational, text-driven editing in place of the direct-manipulation canvas they work in today.

Compass: Continuously Aligning Social Media Feeds via In-Situ Reflections (1 minute read)

Compass is a research system that realigns social media feeds toward users' reflective preferences rather than immediate impulses, surfacing lightweight in-situ notifications during browsing and periodically simulating behavioral signals to reshape feed content. A 10-day YouTube Shorts field study (N=15) found it produced more purposeful consumption and stronger feed alignment without stripping away the casual browsing that makes feeds work.

Enacting Constructive Conflicts with AI Agents to Enhance Reconsideration among Novice Interaction Designers (1 minute read)

A study of 45 design students found that AI agents built to enact "constructive conflict," synthesizing stakeholder pushback rather than adding ideas, drove novice interaction designers to reconsider and revise their proposals more than solo reflection or written prompts alone. The antagonistic agent surfaced more conflicting perspectives than a self-guided framework and pushed participants to generate and discard more ideas, turning reconsideration into concrete design changes.

LEGOUI: Designing with UI-DSL Bricks to Balance Transparency and Controllability (1 minute read)

LegoUI is a staged generative UI framework that breaks interface design into sequential, interpretable steps, capturing each in a UI domain-specific language with provenance so designers can see the model's reasoning and intervene between stages. Across 40 real-world prompts its requirement-analysis stage hit over 95% accuracy with near-complete coverage, and user-study participants reported greater transparency and control than one-shot generative tools.
