RTA Studio designs mass-timber groundscraper in Auckland as "continuous ribbon" (1 minute read)

RTA Studio completed an 11,500-square-metre mass-timber office in Auckland, using a diagrid structure of locally grown timber to form a curved, low-rise "continuous ribbon" set within a regenerated wetland. The building consolidates offices and research facilities for appliance brands Fisher & Paykel and Haier Australasia into a single campus, part of a broader shift toward timber-diagrid construction for large-footprint workplaces.

Pentagram's Marina Willer celebrates music as an art form in new work for De Bijloke (1 minute read)

Pentagram's Marina Willer redesigned the identity of Ghent's Muziekcentrum De Bijloke, a 13th-century former hospital turned concert hall, into a digital-first "living identity" whose swirling, treble-clef-like marks draw on John Cage's oscilloscope experiments. The system pairs that expressive logo with Forma DJR typography and a palette of muted pinks, teals, purples, and blues pulled from the venue's arched ceilings and stone walls.

I IN clads Human Made store in handcrafted Korean celadon tiles with "powerful presence" (1 minute read)

Tokyo studio I IN clad the new Human Made store in Seoul's Lotte Mall Jamsil in handcrafted Korean celadon tiles, pairing them with an industrial gridded ceiling and playful animal sculptures. The interior translates Nigo's brand ethos, "the future is in the past," into a material contrast that sets the heritage craft of celadon against the department-store context around it.
