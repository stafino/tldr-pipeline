Satyress designs centaur-like robot to give workers "superpowers" for dangerous jobs (1 minute read)

Satyress released Threehalves, a teleoperated centaur-like robot built to let workers handle dangerous jobs from a safe distance, with a wheeled quadruped base supporting a humanoid upper body. Founder Beau Gaston has developed the machine since 2022 and first shared it quietly on X, positioning it against the bipedal-humanoid race as a more stable platform for hazardous industrial work.

Realistic AI architectural renderings must be labelled under EU AI Act (1 minute read)

Realistic AI-generated architectural renderings published in the EU without a disclosure label now risk fines under Article 50 of the EU AI Act, which took effect on 2 August. The rule pulls architecture visualization studios into the same transparency regime as deepfakes, forcing anyone passing off synthetic imagery as real to mark it or face enforcement.

Animating CSS border-image (1 minute read)

Andy Clarke's recent piece revisits CSS border-image to show it can carry gradients and sliced artwork instead of solid or dashed lines, and this tutorial pushes that further into animated borders that travel around a card. The author favors border-image over mask-based approaches for efficiency, since border-image-slice replicates a single gradient slice across all four edges automatically, with the caveat that it won't follow curved corners.

Bottle your judgment, Chat is the wrong UI, Design.md (2 minute read)

Dan Maccarone argues that AI's next frontier for designers is not replacing judgment but productizing it, capturing the method one expert has spent a career building and handing that decision-making process to anyone. The roundup pairs this with pieces on why chat is the wrong interface for AI agents and how design systems increasingly let designers decide flow while tools resolve the visual details.
