Blocked aria-hidden: The Warning is Right, and Every Fix You’ve Found is Wrong (1 minute read)

The blur(), setTimeout, and aria-hidden-removal fixes that top every search result for the "Blocked aria-hidden on an element because its descendant retained focus" warning all silence the console while stranding screen-reader users whose focus falls into a hidden region. The author's rule is a reordering, not a rewrite: move focus out of a region before hiding or inert-ing it, or migrate to the native <dialog> element and let showModal() run the focus dance for you.
