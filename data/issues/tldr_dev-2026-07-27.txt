New GitHub, PyPI Policies Boost Supply Chain Security (1 minute read)

GitHub and PyPI shipped new supply-chain defenses: Dependabot now waits three days before opening PRs for non-security version bumps, and PyPI will block file uploads to releases older than 14 days. Both target the same attack window, giving scanners and maintainers time to catch poisoned packages before they propagate and preventing compromised tokens from tampering with long-stable releases.
