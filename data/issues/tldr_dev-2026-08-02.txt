F*: A general-purpose proof-oriented programming language (1 minute read)

F* is a general-purpose proof-oriented programming language from Microsoft Research and Inria that pairs dependent types with SMT-based proof automation and tactic-driven interactive theorem proving. Programs compile to OCaml by default, with fragments extractable to F#, C, Wasm, or assembly, and the language already backs verified cryptography libraries like HACL* and EverCrypt in Project Everest.
