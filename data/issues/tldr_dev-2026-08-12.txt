Blocked aria-hidden: The Warning is Right, and Every Fix You’ve Found is Wrong (1 minute read)

The blocked aria-hidden console warning appears identically across Angular, Bootstrap, Ionic, and phpMyAdmin, and the common fixes (blur(), setTimeout, or stripping the attribute) all silence it while dropping screen reader focus into a hidden region. The real fix is an order-of-operations one: focus must leave a region before it becomes hidden or inert, and migrating to the native <dialog> element with .showModal() hands the entire focus dance to the browser.

Why Tiny JPEGs Look Different in Chrome (1 minute read)

Chrome speeds up rendering of downscaled JPEGs by decoding directly at a reduced scale rather than fully decompressing and then shrinking, exploiting the fact that heavy downscaling mostly discards high-frequency detail. The author traces a logo that looked thicker in Chrome than Firefox at 15px to this DCT-level optimization, walking through how JPEG stores frequency data and why the shortcut changes the result.
