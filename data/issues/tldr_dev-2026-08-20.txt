Don't be a meat proxy (1 minute read)

The author argues that pasting an LLM's raw output into Slack, PR reviews, or group chats adds no value, since anyone can query the model themselves and control their own context. He contends the actual work is reading, understanding, and validating the output, then rewriting it in your own words, so acting as a "meat proxy" that relays verbose, jargon-dense, often plausibly wrong text just shifts the effort onto the reader.

Over 2,500 Organizations Impacted by LiteLLM Supply Chain Attack (1 minute read)

A supply chain attack on the LiteLLM Python library exposed an estimated 434,000 CI/CD pipelines and over 2,500 organizations, including Nvidia, AWS, and the London Stock Exchange Group, after two malicious versions reached PyPI. LiteLLM was compromised indirectly when its CI pipeline auto-installed a poisoned Trivy build, and although the tainted packages were live for only 40 minutes, the payload ran on every Python invocation and leaked cloud keys, SSH keys, and tokens.
