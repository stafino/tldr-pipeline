One fallen power line exposed a growing AI data center problem. Here’s how to fix it. (1 minute read)

A downed power line outside Washington, DC destabilized PJM's grid for over 11 minutes when roughly 3.1 gigawatts of Northern Virginia data centers switched to backup power almost simultaneously, spiking voltage from Virginia to Chicago. The synchronized load-shedding, which represented about 3% of PJM demand, points to a design problem operators expect to recur as data centers must learn to ride through supply disruptions rather than instantly disconnecting.
