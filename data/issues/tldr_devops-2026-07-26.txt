Systems and Delays (1 minute read)

The author works through the delay chapter of Donald Meadows' "Thinking in Systems," using a car-dealership inventory model to show how feedback loops with lag produce overshoot and oscillation rather than smooth convergence. The operational parallel is direct: autoscalers, cache warm-up, and capacity planning all react to signals that arrive late, so aggressive correction amplifies the swings it means to dampen. The takeaway is to tune control loops for the delay, not the current gap.
