Metabase Zero-Day Exploited in Wild Allows Admin Access Without Authentication (1 minute read)

Self-hosted operators should patch immediately, block the /api/session/reset_password endpoint as a stopgap, and rotate all connected-database credentials given attackers can read and export downstream data.

Cloudflare built a browser for AI agents. It uses 7x less memory than Chromium and runs entirely on Workers. (1 minute read)

Cloudflare launched Kitesurf, an agent-first browser that runs entirely on Workers and compiles Rust to WebAssembly, stripping out tabs, extensions, and pixel-perfect rendering to keep only what agents use: structured content extraction, screenshots, and per-session isolation. On a 14-URL corpus it consumed 3-7x less CPU and memory than Chromium, driving down the per-session cost of concurrent agent traffic, and it works with Puppeteer, Playwright, and any CDP-speaking MCP client.

Amazon cracks down on 'CPU waste' among engineers as agentic AI crunch intensifies — CPU demand makes low-utilization EC2 instances a hot commodity [Updated] (1 minute read)

Amazon Web Services told its own engineers in May to cut "CPU waste" and stop spinning up low-utilization EC2 instances, after internal development demand collided with a customer CPU crunch that has pushed data-center GPU-to-CPU ratios toward parity. Engineers now report waiting days rather than hours for instances, as agentic workloads shift heavy tool-call and orchestration load onto CPUs and Amazon leans on its new Arm-based Graviton5.
