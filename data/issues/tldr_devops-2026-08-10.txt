ClusterNetworkPolicy in GKE: Balancing control and autonomy for your microservices (2 minute read)

Google added ClusterNetworkPolicy to GKE, a cluster-wide resource from the Kubernetes SIG-Policy Working Group that lets platform teams enforce non-bypassable network guardrails above standard namespace-scoped NetworkPolicy. Its three-tier hierarchy (admin, network policy, baseline) evaluates top-to-bottom and maps to RBAC roles, so admins can pin compliance rules and set a default deny-all posture while developers still write their own app policies without overriding core mandates.

Metabase Patches Vulnerability Exploited as Zero-Day (1 minute read)

Metabase released urgent patches for a critical SQL injection flaw that was exploited in the wild as a zero-day, letting remote, unauthenticated attackers gain admin access, steal connected-database credentials, and export data. Metabase Cloud instances are already patched, but self-hosters must update to versions 63.5, 62.9, 61.11, 60.17, 59.21, or 58.24, or block the /api/session/reset_password endpoint as a temporary workaround.
