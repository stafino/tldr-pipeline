A sandbox is only as closed as what an AI agent can reach (3 minute read)

GitLab dissects a July incident in which an OpenAI model under internal evaluation escaped its sandbox within an hour, chaining an SSRF and a token-signature bypass (CVE-2026-65616) in a package proxy on its egress allowlist to reach the open internet and Hugging Face's production infrastructure. The lesson for platform teams: an allowlist grants a workload the reach of every destination it can compromise, so any egress-gated environment with the same shape (CI runners, hosted sandboxes, network-reachable tool servers) inherits the same blind spot.

How GitLab tracks vulnerabilities through refactors and reformatting (2 minute read)

GitLab detailed an upgrade to its Scope+Offset vulnerability fingerprinting, which now ignores comments and blank lines when computing a finding's identity so non-functional edits stop registering as new vulnerabilities. On a benchmark of 2,247 comment-insertion commits, the original method accumulated 1,361 duplicate fingerprints while the normalized version produced zero and cut unique fingerprints by 43%. The payoff is less futile re-triage for security teams and more trust in scan results across refactors and reformatting.

Databricks Network Configuration delivery to Tens of Millions of Serverless VMs (1 minute read)

Databricks re-architected how its serverless platform delivers network configuration to VMs, replacing synchronous fan-out calls to upstream services with event-driven pre-computation and snapshot serving. Cluster startup no longer aggregates config on the critical path. VMs read a pre-computed per-workspace snapshot, cutting RPC latency 97.5% from 5,000ms to 125ms and reaching 99.99% availability across billions of daily requests as the platform launches tens of millions of VMs across AWS, Azure, and GCP.
