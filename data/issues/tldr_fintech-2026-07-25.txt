Russia’s largest bank Sberbank plans crypto trading infrastructure by December (5 minute read)

Sberbank, Russia's largest bank, plans to launch crypto trading infrastructure and a digital depository by Dec. 1, following a new law that brings crypto trading, custody, and settlement into the country's regulated financial system starting Sept. 1. Public exchange trading will be limited to assets clearing Bank of Russia liquidity thresholds, licensed-intermediary rules apply from July 2027, and crypto payments for goods and services inside Russia remain prohibited.

Robinhood Chain's real-world assets jump fivefold as tokenized stocks start trading in bigger size (5 minute read)

Tokenized real-world assets on Robinhood Chain surged roughly fivefold to about $70M in under two weeks, as a dozen tokenized stocks led by GameStop, Nvidia, and SpaceX each cleared over $500,000 in daily volume. Total value locked tripled to about $312M since mid-July, though stablecoins and memecoin trading still dominate the chain, making the shift toward equities onchain real but only partial.
