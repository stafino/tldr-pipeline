South Korea’s KB Kookmin Bank to launch cross-border payment service on JPMorgan’s Kinexys: report (1 minute read)

KB Kookmin Bank, South Korea's largest bank, will launch a blockchain-based cross-border corporate payments service next month on JPMorgan's Kinexys network, becoming the first Korean institution to settle import and export transactions on the rail. The service starts with US dollar transfers across 10 countries through the bank's domestic and Singapore branches, extending Kinexys' near-real-time institutional settlement into Korean trade finance.

South Korea trading giant puts receivables onchain in tokenization test with LG CNS (5 minute read)

POSCO International, South Korea's largest trading company, is tokenizing live trade receivables on the Injective layer-1 blockchain in a pilot with LG CNS, replacing the separate ledgers buyers, sellers, and banks reconcile over days with a single transferable record that carries compliance rules with the asset. POSCO, which booked $22.2B in revenue last year, plans to move the system into production after the pilot to speed settlement between its global subsidiaries.

Chime launches in-app investing (5 minute read)

Chime launched Chime Invest, letting users trade stocks and ETFs commission-free or hold an Atomic Invest-managed portfolio with no account minimums and management fees ranging from 0% to 0.25% by membership tier. The move deepens Chime's push to rebundle banking, investing, and lending in one app, keeping the roughly 50 monthly transactions per member inside its ecosystem rather than a third-party brokerage.

Marqeta partners with Zerohash for stablecoin-linked cards (5 minute read)

Marqeta partnered with Zerohash to let any of its customers embed stablecoin-linked cards into new or existing card programs without rebuilding core systems or taking on new regulatory burden. The move gives Marqeta's thousands of live programs a switch-on path to stablecoin spend, but its US model requires a sponsor bank that takes an interchange cut, unlike Puerto Rico-based rival Rain, which issues directly as a Visa principal member.

Intuit launches business credit card that syncs natively with QuickBooks (5 minute read)

Intuit launched a business credit card that syncs transactions natively with QuickBooks, auto-categorizing spend and reconciling against the books in real time rather than through periodic feed imports. The card leans on Intuit's underwriting via QuickBooks financial data, targeting small businesses already inside the accounting platform and tightening its grip on the SMB money-movement stack.

Ramp launches AI token spend controls (5 minute read)

Ramp launched AI Token Spend Management, a dashboard that lets finance teams track and cap AI token and subscription spend across Anthropic, OpenAI, Cursor, and Gemini in one place. The tool ties usage to cost, flags savings and anomalies, and sets limits by team, project, or API key, addressing a category that has grown 20.7x across Ramp customers since June 2025.

Ant International raises $1.2B for global expansion (5 minute read)

Ant International raised $1.2B to fund global expansion, deepening its push into cross-border payments, treasury management, and merchant acquiring outside China. The round positions the Ant Group affiliate to compete more directly with Stripe, Adyen, and Wise on international settlement rails, particularly across Southeast Asia and emerging markets where it already processes large wallet-to-wallet volumes.

Nubank to acquire Banco Porto Real de Investimentos, strengthen Brazil operation (5 minute read)

Nubank agreed to acquire Banco Porto Real de Investimentos, a Rio de Janeiro wholesale-credit bank, adding its banking license to Nubank's existing Brazilian licenses pending Central Bank approval. The new license carries no additional capital or liquidity requirements and leaves service unchanged for Nubank's 115 million customers, deepening its position as Brazil's largest private financial institution by customer count.

Payments M&A spools up (5 minute read)

Payments M&A activity is accelerating in 2026, with strategic acquirers and private-equity buyers moving on processors, gateways, and infrastructure providers after a multi-year lull in dealmaking. The Funding: Are crypto vaults funds and curators fund managers? (1 minute read)

SEC Commissioner Hester Peirce published a statement arguing that crypto vaults and onchain lending strategies may already fall under federal securities laws, warning that moving an activity onchain does not exempt it. The statement, the second time in three months SEC leadership has addressed vaults, raises the prospect that curators running them could be treated as fund managers subject to registration under the Securities Act and Advisers Act.

EDX Markets Closes $76M Series C Led by SBI Holdings (1 minute read)

EDX Markets, an institution-only crypto trading venue with its own central clearinghouse, closed a $76M Series C led by SBI Holdings, making the Tokyo-listed financial group a strategic investor in the US exchange. The raise funds expansion of EDX's clearing and settlement capabilities and follows its OCC application to charter EDX Trust, a proposed national trust bank for regulated custody and settlement.

Profitable Serbian-Hungarian InsurTech Ominimo hits unicorn status two years after launch (1 minute read)

Ominimo, a profitable Serbian-Hungarian motor-insurance startup, raised €20.1M at a €1.4B valuation from the EBRD's venture arm, reaching unicorn status two years after its 2024 launch. The company's data-driven pricing pushed its annualized gross written premium run-rate from €26.3M in 2024 to roughly €306.8M today, and it plans to secure its own insurance licence and enter the US in 2027.

Big banks' record Wall Street profits are increasingly tied to AI (5 minute read)

The five largest Wall Street banks reported $114B in capital markets revenue for the first half of 2026, up 31.5% year-over-year, with stock trading tied to the AI boom driving more than half the gain. AI is fueling underwriting fees, equities trading, and wealth inflows from IPO employees, though executives including Jamie Dimon warned the trading haul is "close to as good as it gets.

Augustus secures $180 million Series B (5 minute read)

Augustus raised $180M in a Series B at a $1B valuation, led by Tiger Global with the founders of Nubank, Ramp, Circle, and Deel participating, to build a federally chartered bank giving international fintechs and banks direct access to USD accounts and payment rails. By pursuing a charter rather than renting one, Augustus is positioning to disintermediate the sponsor-bank layer that firms outside the US currently depend on for dollar clearing.

SoftBank’s $40 Billion Loan for OpenAI Stake Gets 21 New Lenders (1 minute read)

SoftBank's $40B bridge loan financing its OpenAI investment has drawn 21 additional lenders in a broader syndication phase, spreading exposure to one of the largest single-name tech bets on record. The widening lender group signals banks are willing to underwrite concentrated AI-infrastructure risk, even as SoftBank leans on debt rather than balance-sheet cash to fund the stake.

HSBC to Hire 100 AI Specialists as Bank Sets Up Singapore Center (1 minute read)

The move positions Singapore as a hub for HSBC's AI buildout and signals that large banks are now competing directly with fintechs and tech firms for scarce machine-learning talent.
