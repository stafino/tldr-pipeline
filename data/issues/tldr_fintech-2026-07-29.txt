10 European Banks Launch RL1 Blockchain Cooperative (1 minute read)

Ten European financial institutions, including ABN AMRO, DZ BANK and Natixis CIB, launched Regulated Layer One, a jointly owned, permissioned blockchain for digital bond issuance, tokenized real-world assets, and repo and derivatives margining, domiciled in Luxembourg as a cooperative with one vote per member. The network, built on SWIAT's BaFin-supervised infrastructure, has run in production for three years and settled over 700 million euros ($815M) across regulated financial markets.

SoFi Says SoFiUSD Settlement Now Live on Q2 Earnings Call (1 minute read)

SoFi confirmed on its Q2 earnings call that commercial clients are now settling transactions in real time through SoFiUSD, moving the stablecoin from launch to a live production rail seven months after SoFi became the first nationally chartered US bank to issue a stablecoin on a public blockchain. The enterprise-focused rail runs 24/7 through the SoFi Exchange Network and is slated to plug into Mastercard's global payments network.

Tether’s GENIUS-compliant USAT stablecoin launches on Celo, marking first expansion beyond Ethereum (1 minute read)

Tether launched USAT, its GENIUS Act-compliant stablecoin issued by OCC-chartered Anchorage Digital Bank, on Celo, its first mainnet deployment beyond Ethereum since going live in January. Celo's CIP-64 fee abstraction lets users pay gas directly in USAT rather than holding CELO, and the network already handles 28% of cross-chain USDT transfers, giving Tether's regulated dollar a high-volume distribution base.

MoonPay launches PayBox, an AI payment vault for ChatGPT and Claude (1 minute read)

MoonPay launched PayBox, a non-custodial payment vault that connects to ChatGPT and Claude, letting the assistant prepare transactions like flight bookings and restaurant reservations that users approve with a passkey before funds move. Built on the x402 agent-payment standard now backed by Visa, Mastercard, and Stripe, PayBox combines crypto wallets and cards across Solana and EVM chains, with an autonomous mode for spending within user-set limits.

Stablecoin firm Brale says new protocol can remove a major hurdle to scaling custom tokens (5 minute read)

Brale introduced ION Protocol on testnet, an interoperability system that moves participating stablecoins across blockchains by burning tokens on one chain and minting them on another, without the pre-funded liquidity pools most bridges require. The launch targets fragmentation in a stablecoin market that now spans more than 350 tokens and over $300B in market cap, cutting the capital issuers must lock up on every supported chain.

Coalition of 44 state attorneys general says CFTC lacks authority over sports prediction markets (1 minute read)

A coalition of 44 state attorneys general sent a letter to the CFTC arguing the agency lacks authority over sports prediction markets and urging it to draft new rules consistent with the Commodity Exchange Act. The pushback raises the odds of state-by-state enforcement against platforms like Kalshi and Polymarket, complicating the regulatory path for event-contract fintechs that have leaned on federal preemption.

PayPal turned down $53bn, and pointed to a beat and a $70 target (1 minute read)

PayPal publicly rejected Stripe and Advent's $60.50-a-share bid, roughly $53bn, on its Q2 earnings call, where CEO Enrique Lores said the offer failed to clear its bar for shareholder value and pointed to Cantor's $70 valuation against a stock near $58. The rebuff rode a beat, with revenue up 5% to $8.68bn and raised full-year guidance, though transaction-margin dollars grew just 1% as easing rates cut interest on customer balances.

Software Woes Push Europe Lenders to Seek Rare Repayment Terms (1 minute read)

European lenders are demanding rapid-amortization and stricter repayment terms on loans to software companies, structures rarely used since the 2008 financial crisis, on fears that AI will erode those firms' revenue. The shift signals that credit markets now price generative AI as a direct threat to software business models, raising borrowing costs for a sector that has long enjoyed cheap, covenant-light debt.

BNY targets $8.6 trillion transfer agency market on blockchain rails (5 minute read)

BNY is moving its transfer agency record-keeping, which services roughly $8.6T across 7.6M accounts, onto blockchain to create a single on-chain ownership ledger for tokenized funds and cut out intermediaries. Baillie Gifford, BlackRock, and BNY's Dreyfus unit are the first clients, though the 242-year-old custodian expects traditional rails to coexist for years given cyber and smart-contract risks.
