Amazon Business adds Affirm as its first BNPL option at checkout (5 minute read)

Amazon Business added Affirm as its first buy-now-pay-later option at checkout, marking the launch of Affirm's new B2B pay-over-time product for sole proprietors. Eligible customers get an instant credit decision and can split eligible purchases across three to 48 months with no late or hidden fees, rolling out now and reaching all eligible sole proprietors by Black Friday.

Visa used Mythos to hunt for bugs in its own payment network, then open-sourced the harness that made it possible (2 minute read)

Visa aimed Anthropic's Claude Mythos at the infrastructure behind billions of daily transactions and had the model chain minor weaknesses deep in its stack into working exploits that normally surface only late in penetration testing. The company open-sourced the Visa Vulnerability Agentic Harness that governed the hunt on GitHub, alongside a white paper detailing the architecture and 12 architectural practices any security team can adapt for critical infrastructure.

Marqeta partners with Zerohash for stablecoin-linked cards (5 minute read)

Marqeta partnered with Zerohash to let any of its customers embed stablecoin-linked cards into new or existing card programs without rebuilding core systems or taking on new regulatory burden. The move gives Marqeta's thousands of live programs a switch-on path to stablecoin spend, but its US model requires a sponsor bank that takes an interchange cut, unlike Puerto Rico-based rival Rain, which issues directly as a Visa principal member.

Intuit launches business credit card that syncs natively with QuickBooks (5 minute read)

Intuit launched a business credit card that syncs transactions natively with QuickBooks, auto-categorizing spend and reconciling against the books in real time rather than through periodic feed imports. The card leans on Intuit's underwriting via QuickBooks financial data, targeting small businesses already inside the accounting platform and tightening its grip on the SMB money-movement stack.

Ramp launches AI token spend controls (5 minute read)

Ramp launched AI Token Spend Management, a dashboard that lets finance teams track and cap AI token and subscription spend across Anthropic, OpenAI, Cursor, and Gemini in one place. The tool ties usage to cost, flags savings and anomalies, and sets limits by team, project, or API key, addressing a category that has grown 20.7x across Ramp customers since June 2025.

Ant International raises $1.2B for global expansion (5 minute read)

Ant International raised $1.2B to fund global expansion, deepening its push into cross-border payments, treasury management, and merchant acquiring outside China. The round positions the Ant Group affiliate to compete more directly with Stripe, Adyen, and Wise on international settlement rails, particularly across Southeast Asia and emerging markets where it already processes large wallet-to-wallet volumes.

PayPal tops Q2 estimates and raises full-year forecast amid Stripe takeover bid (1 minute read)

PayPal reported Q2 revenue of $8.68B and adjusted EPS of $1.38, beating estimates, and raised its full-year adjusted EPS forecast to about $5.38, less than two weeks after its board deemed a $53B Stripe-Advent buyout offer too low. Total payment volume rose 10% to $486.4B and branded checkout grew 2% for a second straight quarter, giving CEO Enrique Lores a case that his turnaround is working as the takeover fight plays out.

Nubank to acquire Banco Porto Real de Investimentos, strengthen Brazil operation (5 minute read)

Nubank agreed to acquire Banco Porto Real de Investimentos, a Rio de Janeiro wholesale-credit bank, adding its banking license to Nubank's existing Brazilian licenses pending Central Bank approval. The new license carries no additional capital or liquidity requirements and leaves service unchanged for Nubank's 115 million customers, deepening its position as Brazil's largest private financial institution by customer count.

Augustus secures $180 million Series B (5 minute read)

Augustus raised $180M in a Series B at a $1B valuation, led by Tiger Global with the founders of Nubank, Ramp, Circle, and Deel participating, to build a federally chartered bank giving international fintechs and banks direct access to USD accounts and payment rails. By pursuing a charter rather than renting one, Augustus is positioning to disintermediate the sponsor-bank layer that firms outside the US currently depend on for dollar clearing.
