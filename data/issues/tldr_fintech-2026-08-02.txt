Iran-linked exchange sent $676 million to Binance wallets in alleged sanctions-evasion operation: Reuters (1 minute read)

Roughly $540 million of that reached Binance after Dubai regulators fined the unlicensed operator in January 2025, underscoring how sanctioned flows keep reaching major exchanges despite enforcement.
