The $114 Trillion Question: How DTCC Is Tokenizing the Entire U.S. Market (1 minute read)

The pilot signals the largest US clearing and settlement infrastructure is moving toward tokenized securities, which would pull on-chain settlement from fintech experiments into the core plumbing of American capital markets.

The billion-dollar question: How will the neolabs actually make money? (5 minute read)

The author examines how neolabs, the new wave of AI-native research startups, will convert frontier models into durable revenue rather than perpetual burn. The piece argues that API access and per-token pricing alone won't cover training costs, pushing labs toward vertical applications, enterprise contracts, and infrastructure lock-in. For fintech operators building on top of these models, the unresolved question is who ultimately captures the margin as inference costs and pricing power shift.
