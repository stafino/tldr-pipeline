Mastercard Extends Crypto Credential to Cross-Border Stablecoin Payments in Borderless.xyz Pilot (1 minute read)

Mastercard and Borderless.xyz began piloting Mastercard Crypto Credential for cross-border stablecoin payments, using the network's verification system to attest that a provider has passed a compliant KYC/AML program so counterparties can trust it without re-running checks. The single-audit model targets the core scaling problem in stablecoin payments, where every new counterparty relationship currently triggers fresh compliance work, and launches with live transactions across Infinia, Walapay, and Koywe.

Tempo plans to support BlackRock's new money market fund (5 minute read)

BlackRock named Tempo as a supported blockchain for BRSRV, a proposed U.S. government money market fund whose OnChain Shares are digitally represented on public networks and administered by Securitize as transfer agent. The fund aims to have its shares qualify as eligible reserve assets for GENIUS Act stablecoin issuers, connecting regulated cash management to the same rails institutions use for onchain payments and settlement.

Dinari Opens 724 Tokenized US Stocks to Eligible US Investors (1 minute read)

Dinari opened tokenized trading of 724 US stocks and ETFs, including the full S&P 500, to eligible US investors and businesses, with purchases settled in USDC via Circle and backing securities held in qualified custody. The dShares tokens preserve voting rights, dividends, and best-execution protections, add native USDC dividend payouts, and run on Ethereum, Avalanche, Arbitrum, and Base, following Dinari's June 2025 broker-dealer approval.

CME, FanDuel Venture Falters as Prediction Startups Race Ahead (1 minute read)

CME Group and FanDuel are scaling back their joint prediction-market venture after failing to gain traction against fast-growing startups like Kalshi and Polymarket. The retreat signals how quickly event-contract platforms have become a competitive threat to both established derivatives exchanges and traditional sportsbooks, pulling regulated volume toward CFTC-overseen prediction markets that neither incumbent has managed to counter.

Kalshi Not Running 'a Casino' Says Head of Enforcement (1 minute read)

Kalshi's head of enforcement, Robert DeNault, publicly rejected New York Attorney General Letitia James's lawsuit against the prediction-market platform, arguing Kalshi operates open marketplaces rather than a casino or sportsbook and warning of regulatory overreach. The dispute sharpens the unsettled question of whether event-contract platforms fall under state gambling law or federal CFTC oversight, a jurisdictional fight that will shape how prediction markets scale in the US.
