Black Lake Launches Harbor Verify to Bring Verifiable Credit to Onchain Markets (1 minute read)

Black Lake Digital Markets launched Harbor Verify, a browser-based tool that lets investors, lenders, auditors, and rating agencies cryptographically confirm every loan in a tokenized pool meets its published eligibility rules without seeing private borrower data. The tool combines onchain rulebook hashes, Merkle commitments, and zero-knowledge proofs, and is going live first on a $25M residential mortgage portfolio, replacing bilateral reports and restricted data rooms with independently testable proofs.

Google says hackers are calling financial firm employees to hack and extort victims (1 minute read)
