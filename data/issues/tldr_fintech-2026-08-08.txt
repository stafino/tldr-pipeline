New XRP Ledger amendments target $530 million in tokenized Wall Street assets (5 minute read)

Ripple released XRP Ledger version 3.3.0, which proposes a "Confidential Transfers" amendment that encrypts balances and payment amounts on Multi-Purpose Tokens while keeping accounts and token types visible to issuers, auditors, and regulators. The privacy feature targets the roughly $1.38B in tokenized real-world assets already issued on XRPL, but it and five other institutional amendments need two weeks of 80% validator support before going live.

The US OCC denies Dutch fintech Bunq's application for a national bank charter, citing the need for more clarity on its business plan to expand in the US (Bloomberg) (1 minute read)

The OCC denied Dutch fintech Bunq's application for a US national bank charter, citing insufficient clarity on its business plan for American expansion. The rejection forces Bunq to either resubmit with a sharper US strategy or fall back on a sponsor-bank arrangement, extending the pattern of foreign neobanks struggling to secure direct federal charters.

Majority Leader Thune files cloture on Clarity Act, setting up Sept. 15 Senate vote (1 minute read)

Senate Majority Leader John Thune filed cloture on the motion to proceed to the Clarity Act, the crypto market-structure bill, setting a Sept. 15 Senate floor vote when lawmakers return from recess. Cloture needs 60 votes and would only open debate, not pass the bill, and negotiators have yet to resolve disputes over ethics clauses, illicit-finance provisions, and how Senate Agriculture Committee text folds in.

The turmoil at one of Europe's better-known corporate-card and expense platforms raises questions about its independence and whether a founder-era growth story can survive a leadership reset and a tighter fundraising market.

Crypto cards hit $759 million a month (1 minute read)

Crypto payment card volume reached $759M in July, up roughly 2.5x from $306M a year earlier, with USDC and USDT now handling 84% of spend as euro-backed stablecoins collapsed from 88% to 2% of volume. The activity settles overwhelmingly through Visa and skews to dollar rails, giving stablecoin holders card-network access without a bank account and pushing digital dollars into everyday point-of-sale spend.

Brazil's central bank orders exchanges to delay large crypto transfers abroad (5 minute read)

Brazil's central bank issued Resolution BCB No. 584/2026, requiring crypto exchanges to hold customer transfers above $10,000 to foreign platforms or self-custody wallets for up to 24 hours starting Jan. 1, 2027. The measure targets crypto and stablecoins used to launder fraud proceeds, but shifts risk-scoring duties onto exchanges and could raise costs for legitimate users and dent domestic platforms' competitiveness.

Private Credit Squeezed By Bank Refinancings: Credit Weekly (1 minute read)

Highly indebted companies are increasingly refinancing out of private credit loans into the cheaper syndicated bank loan market, reversing years of share gains by direct lenders. The shift exposes private credit's pricing disadvantage as higher-for-longer rates make bank financing relatively attractive, pressuring the spreads and deal flow that fueled the asset class's rapid growth.
