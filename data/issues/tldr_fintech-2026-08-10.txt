The Deluge of AI Debt Has Big Names Seeking New Strategies to Avoid Selloffs (1 minute read)

BlackRock structured its financing of Meta's AI data-center buildout to deter fast-trading investors, part of a broader push by big underwriters to manage a flood of AI-related debt hitting credit markets. As issuers stack tens of billions in bonds and private credit to fund compute expansion, banks and asset managers are engineering new deal structures to keep spreads stable and head off a selloff.

AI Cloud Provider Lambda Taps Loans for Nvidia-Tied Chip Deal (1 minute read)

Lambda, the Nvidia-backed AI cloud provider, is selling a leveraged loan to finance a chip purchase, pushing the buildout into the riskier corners of the debt market. The deal signals that AI infrastructure spending has outrun equity and vendor financing, with leveraged loans now a growing funding channel for compute-hungry providers stretching to secure Nvidia GPUs.

Brazil to tighten crypto fraud controls with new 24-hour wait on transfers to self-custody wallets (1 minute read)

Brazil will require a 24-hour holding period on crypto transfers from exchanges to self-custody wallets starting Jan. 1, 2027, covering fiat-backed stablecoins as part of a new anti-fraud regime. The delay gives platforms and victims a window to flag and reverse scam-driven withdrawals, but it adds settlement friction that stablecoin and payments operators serving Brazilian users will need to design around.

Why the UK financial watchdog is drafting new rules for tokenized gold (5 minute read)

The FCA is drafting rules for tokenized gold and consulting financial institutions on how bullion-backed tokens could serve as collateral in wholesale markets, with progress expected within months. The move is part of a broader UK push to digitize wholesale markets, projected to add £33B in annual output, as London defends its 70% share of global gold trading against rising competition from China.

Podcast: AI Distribution for 5000+ Banks, with Fiserv Co-Head of Financial Solutions Srini Krish (1 minute read)

Fiserv is embedding AI into the technology backbone it provides to 5,000+ US banks and credit unions, packaging models from OpenAI and Anthropic into its Agent OS platform so smaller institutions can compete with JPMorgan and Wells Fargo. The rollout starts with non-real-time, human-in-the-loop cases like commercial loan underwriting, on the logic that money demands deterministic outputs and Fiserv's real moat is distribution rather than direct model access.

FCA selects five fintechs to support its Scale-up Unit (1 minute read)

The FCA selected five UK fintechs (ClearScore, Modulr, Teya, Urban Jungle, and Zilch) for the latest cohort of its Scale-up Unit, the first group regulated solely by the FCA to receive dedicated scaleup support spanning payments, consumer credit, credit scoring, and insurtech. The regulator paired the announcement with pilot findings arguing that early investment in regulatory risk and control frameworks is critical to sustainable growth.
