Inside stablecoin firm BVNK’s journey to a $1.8B acquisition by Mastercard (5 minute read)

Mastercard closed its $1.8B acquisition of stablecoin infrastructure firm BVNK, beating out competing bids from Coinbase and Visa despite Coinbase reportedly offering more. The deal reflects how Stripe, Visa, Mastercard, and Coinbase are racing to lock up positions in the roughly $300B stablecoin market, with incumbent networks buying rails rather than building them.

Revolut clinches French banking licence (1 minute read)

Revolut secured a French banking licence from the ACPR and the ECB, adding to its Lithuanian-issued European licence and clearing the way to offer locally tailored products like loans to its 30M Western European customers. The approval follows last year's ECB restrictions over Revolut's product-approval controls and precedes a €1B regional investment and a Paris HQ, with customers migrating to the new licence starting in France.

Sources: the UK FCA is consulting industry participants, including major banks, on a regulatory framework for tokenized gold; London dominates the global market (Financial Times) (1 minute read)

The UK's Financial Conduct Authority is consulting banks and other industry participants on a regulatory framework for tokenized gold, according to the Financial Times. London already dominates the global gold market, and clear rules on tokenized reserves would give the FCA an early lead in setting standards for a fast-growing corner of real-world-asset tokenization.

Rakuten Earns First Profit in Six Years With Fintech Boost (1 minute read)

Rakuten Group posted its first quarterly net income in six years, driven by growth in its fintech and e-commerce segments. The result underscores how Rakuten's payments, banking, and credit-card arms have become the profit engine offsetting years of losses from its mobile-carrier buildout.

India’s UPI Payments Developer Targets Diaspora For Growth (1 minute read)

NPCI, the operator of India's UPI real-time payments network, plans to expand globally by targeting the country's overseas diaspora as a beachhead for cross-border transactions. The move positions UPI to compete with correspondent-banking rails and remittance incumbents on the corridors where Indian expats send money home, pressuring settlement costs and timing in one of the world's largest remittance markets.

How credit card issuers and reservation apps like OpenTable and Amex-owned Resy fragment restaurant reservations, wiring the best tables into premium card perks (Wall Street Journal) (1 minute read)

Credit card issuers and reservation platforms including OpenTable and Amex-owned Resy are wiring the best restaurant tables into premium card perks, fragmenting availability across booking apps tied to specific cards. The shift turns dining reservations into a customer-acquisition and retention lever for issuers, deepening the link between high-annual-fee cards and exclusive access as premium-card competition intensifies.

DORA Is No Longer a Deadline (1 minute read)

The European Supervisory Authorities published their first DORA incident overview on June 3, 2026, ending the informal supervisory tolerance of 2025 as national authorities begin auto-cross-checking Register of Information submissions and issuing initial compulsion payments. Enforcement is arriving unevenly, with penalty ceilings ranging from Italy's €20M or 10% of turnover down to the Czech Republic's €2M, even as Poland and Bulgaria have yet to fully transpose DORA into national law.
