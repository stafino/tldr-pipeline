Standard Chartered-backed Anchorpoint begins HKDAP stablecoin rollout (1 minute read)

The issuer is targeting cross-border payments and the settlement and distribution of tokenized real-world assets first, with HashKey Exchange joining as a distribution partner after completing its first HKDAP mint and redemption.

MoneyGram Brings Cash-To-Crypto Ramps To Solana (1 minute read)

MoneyGram extended MoneyGram Ramps, its cash-to-crypto on- and off-ramp API, natively to Solana, removing the prior requirement to bridge USDC to Stellar before routing users into its retail cash network. Going native on a chain holding $15.73B in stablecoins gives developers direct access to cash deposits in 25+ countries and withdrawals across 170+, backed by nearly 500,000 retail locations, with self-custody app Rift as the first integration.

Trends

Bank of England to test stablecoin, digital currency use in cross-border finance (5 minute read)

The Bank of England moved its digital pound project into Phase 2, testing whether public stablecoins and central bank money can operate together in a single trade-finance payment flow, with NOBO Finance, Dun & Bradstreet, and Polygon Labs joining its Digital Pound Lab. The experiments, which use no real customers or money, will inform how the BOE and Treasury let private stablecoins and a potential CBDC interoperate for cross-border settlement.

CFTC declares market emergency, orders Kalshi to continue to operate in New York (1 minute read)

The CFTC invoked its emergency authority and ordered KalshiEX to keep operating in New York, overriding the state attorney general's bid to shut down its event-contract markets nationwide. The move escalates a jurisdictional fight over whether prediction markets are federally regulated derivatives or state-controlled gambling, with the CFTC now suing nine states to defend its exclusive authority over CFTC-registered exchanges.

MEMX Files to List Prediction Market-Style Bets on Earnings (1 minute read)

MEMX filed with the SEC to list binary contracts on corporate earnings metrics, joining the growing set of exchanges moving to compete with prediction-market platforms like Kalshi and Polymarket. The move pushes event contracts further into regulated exchange territory, testing how the CFTC and SEC divide oversight as earnings-based derivatives blur the line between market data and event wagering.

Revolut clinches French banking licence (1 minute read)

Revolut secured a French banking licence from the ACPR and the ECB, adding to its Lithuanian-issued European licence and clearing the way to offer locally tailored products like loans to its 30M Western European customers. The approval follows last year's ECB restrictions over Revolut's product-approval controls and precedes a €1B regional investment and a Paris HQ, with customers migrating to the new licence starting in France.

Wintermute Plans $1 Billion AI Investment to Compete on Wall Street (1 minute read)

Wintermute Trading plans to invest roughly $1B over the next five years in high-frequency trading and AI data center infrastructure, pushing the crypto market maker into traditional markets after a slump in digital-asset volumes. The move signals that crypto trading firms increasingly see AI compute and HFT infrastructure as the diversification path onto Wall Street, competing directly with established quant shops rather than staying crypto-native.

eToro agrees to buy TradeZero for up to $231 million as equities drive Q2 growth (1 minute read)

eToro agreed to acquire brokerage TradeZero for up to $231 million, expanding its US equities footprint as stock trading fuels its growth. Net contribution rose 9% year-over-year to $229 million in Q2, driven mainly by stronger equities trading, underscoring eToro's push to diversify beyond crypto into commission-based stock and options flow.

India’s central bank wants AI to approve loans that humans would reject (1 minute read)

The Reserve Bank of India proposed guidelines pushing lenders to deploy AI underwriting models that extend credit to thin-file borrowers whom traditional scorecards reject, part of a financial-inclusion push targeting India's large unbanked population. The move puts the RBI in the unusual position of encouraging looser algorithmic lending, raising questions about fair-lending oversight and default exposure as banks adopt models that override human rejection decisions.

New York City Probes Kalshi and Polymarket Over Social Harms (1 minute read)

The New York City Council opened a probe into the advertising practices of Kalshi, Polymarket, and two other prediction-market platforms, examining potential social harms from how the contracts are marketed. The inquiry adds a municipal front to the mounting legal and regulatory pressure on prediction markets, which already face CFTC scrutiny and state-level challenges over whether their event contracts amount to gambling.

Crypto-friendly bank Erebor in talks to raise $1.5 billion at $9.5 billion valuation: FT (5 minute read)

Erebor Bank is in advanced talks to raise about $1.5 billion at a $9.5 billion valuation, per the Financial Times, nearly doubling the year-old lender's private-market value since a $4.35 billion round earlier this year. The fresh capital helps Erebor meet a regulator-mandated 12% leverage ratio as deposits jumped from $1.1 billion in March to $4.6 billion in July across crypto, AI, and defense clients.

The NYC Council sent letters to Polymarket, Kalshi, Coinbase, and Gemini Titan to investigate alleged deceptive marketing practices and targeting minors (Wall Street Journal) (1 minute read)

The New York City Council sent letters to Polymarket, Kalshi, Coinbase, and Gemini demanding information about alleged deceptive marketing practices and the targeting of minors, according to the Wall Street Journal. The inquiry signals mounting municipal scrutiny of prediction-market and crypto platforms, adding a local-government layer of pressure on top of existing CFTC and SEC oversight of how these venues advertise and onboard users.

Fidelity moves to add staking, quarterly payouts to near $900 million ether ETF (5 minute read)

Fidelity filed to add ether staking and quarterly cash payouts to its $898M Fidelity Ethereum Fund (FETH), keeping 85% of gross staking rewards while 15% goes to node operators like Blockdaemon, Figment, and Galaxy. The move follows a November 2025 IRS safe harbor that lets crypto trusts stake without losing grantor-trust tax status, putting Fidelity alongside Grayscale and 21Shares as staking becomes table stakes for spot ether ETFs.

Riot Signs $9.1B, 20-Year AI Data Center Lease at Its Texas Bitcoin Mine (1 minute read)

Riot Platforms leased 191 megawatts at its Rockdale, Texas bitcoin mining campus to an unnamed frontier AI lab under a 20-year agreement Riot values at roughly $9.1B, per an Aug. 10 SEC filing. The deal signals miners repricing cheap, grid-connected power for AI compute: Riot projects the lease to average $457M in annual revenue, four times its entire Q2 mining haul as that business ran at a net loss.

Coinbase Wins Abu Dhabi License for Tokenized Securities Hub (1 minute read)

Coinbase received a Financial Services Permission from Abu Dhabi Global Market's FSRA to build an international tokenization hub, clearing it to arrange and custody tokenized securities backed 1:1 by underlying shares for eligible investors outside the US. Holders will need only a wallet rather than a brokerage or correspondent-banking relationship, though voting and redemption rights are limited to vested holders and every transfer stays subject to sanctions screening with wallet-level freeze capability.

Crypto.com rolls out tokenized stock derivatives as crypto exchanges push into equities (5 minute read)

Crypto.com launched tokenized derivatives tracking 1,500 U.S. stocks and ETFs, including Apple, Nvidia, and Tesla, offering synthetic price exposure without share ownership or voting rights to users in the European Economic Area and other approved markets. The products, issued under a MiFID license from its Foris Capital acquisition, join a tokenized-stock market that has grown roughly 600% to $2.49B in the past year.

CFTC flexes emergency authority to keep Kalshi operating amid New York lawsuit (1 minute read)

The CFTC invoked its emergency authority to order Kalshi to keep operating after New York state sued the prediction-market platform last month over its event contracts. The move asserts federal preemption over state gambling regulators, signaling the CFTC intends to shield CFTC-registered exchanges from state-level challenges as prediction markets expand into sports and election contracts.

Mapping the AI economy (1 minute read)

Stripe published an analysis of its transaction data across thousands of AI companies, finding that the 100 largest by revenue reach 120 markets on average by their third year and draw 48% of revenue from outside their home markets. Beyond high-GDP hubs, India, Mexico, Poland, and the UAE showed outsized AI spend relative to overall Stripe volume, marking them as expansion targets where localized payment methods and pricing convert demand to revenue.

CFTC orders Kalshi to continue offering prediction markets in New York after state lawsuit (5 minute read)

The move escalates a jurisdictional fight over whether prediction markets are federally regulated swaps or state-regulated gaming, with CFTC Chairman Mike Selig arguing that interstate event contracts fall outside New York's gaming laws.

Rain acquired Ansa, a startup whose white-label APIs and SDKs let merchants run their own branded prepaid wallets, with founder Sophia Goldberg joining as head of payments. Rain plans to pair Ansa's stored fiat balances with its Visa and Mastercard issuing so a prefunded balance can spend outside the merchant that loaded it, consolidating wallets, rewards, and stablecoins onto one platform.

Bernstein raised its price target for Riot Platforms to $35 from $30, implying roughly 80% upside, after the bitcoin miner signed a $9.1B, 20-year colocation lease for 191 IT megawatts at its Rockdale, Texas campus with a frontier AI lab reported to be Anthropic.
