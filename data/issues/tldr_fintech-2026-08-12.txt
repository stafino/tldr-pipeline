Kalshi first prediction market to stream full order books via DoubleZero (1 minute read)

Kalshi became the first prediction market to distribute real-time order book data over DoubleZero Edge, a low-latency multicast feed running on dedicated fiber, starting with full depth on its most active sports and crypto-perp contracts. The feed delivers Level 1 and Level 2 data directly, sparing institutional firms from reconstructing the book from API responses, and Kalshi is waiving its revenue share for the first year to lower entry costs.

US tries to override New York gambling laws, orders Kalshi to keep operating (1 minute read)

The CFTC invoked its emergency authority to order Kalshi to keep operating in New York, overriding state Attorney General Letitia James's July 31 lawsuit that seeks to shut the prediction market down as unlicensed gambling. The federal move stakes out the position that event-contract derivatives are interstate financial markets beyond the reach of state gaming laws, setting up a jurisdictional fight that will shape how prediction markets are regulated nationwide.

Kalshi is in advanced talks to raise more than $750M co-led by Sequoia Capital and Wellington Management at a $40B valuation, nearly doubling the $22B mark it hit in May. SEC Advances Tokenized-Securities Exemption That Could Enable 24/7 Trading (1 minute read)

SEC Chair Paul Atkins is advancing an "innovation exemption" for tokenized listed securities, a cabined framework that would let platforms facilitate compliant onchain trading while the Commission drafts long-term rules, and which Bloomberg reports could enable 24/7 stock-token trading. The measure remains pre-release with no specified legal form or effective date, and it is absent from Friday's SEC agenda, which lists only a separate crypto-offerings rule proposal.
