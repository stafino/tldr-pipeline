Klarna and Zilch launch new paid membership packages (1 minute read)

Going native on a chain holding $15.73B in stablecoins gives developers direct access to cash deposits in 25+ countries and withdrawals across 170+, backed by nearly 500,000 retail locations, with self-custody app Rift as the first integration.

CFTC declares market emergency, orders Kalshi to continue to operate in New York (1 minute read)

The CFTC invoked its emergency authority and ordered KalshiEX to keep operating in New York, overriding the state attorney general's bid to shut down its event-contract markets nationwide. The move escalates a jurisdictional fight over whether prediction markets are federally regulated derivatives or state-controlled gambling, with the CFTC now suing nine states to defend its exclusive authority over CFTC-registered exchanges.

MUFG to test real-time blockchain settlement for Japanese government bond trades (5 minute read)

MUFG is preparing a proof-of-concept for onchain settlement of Japanese government bond repo transactions on the Canton network, aiming to compress the current 1-3 day cycle into real-time 24/7 settlement. The move follows JPMorgan's Kinexys network, which has run blockchain-based intraday US Treasury repos since 2020, and fits MUFG's broader push into tokenized finance alongside a planned SMBC-Mizuho stablecoin.

Deutsche Bank becomes first foreign yuan clearing bank in Europe (1 minute read)

The People's Bank of China authorized Deutsche Bank to clear renminbi transactions from Frankfurt, making it the first foreign institution in Europe to hold the designation, a role until now reserved for Chinese lenders like Bank of China and ICBC. The move extends Beijing's overseas yuan-clearing network to a major European bank and gives manufacturers and automakers with Chinese suppliers a direct settlement route that avoids routing payments through the dollar.

SEC Staff Clears Franklin Funds to Use Onchain Money Fund for Cash and Collateral (1 minute read)

SEC staff issued a no-action letter permitting Franklin Templeton's registered funds to hold shares of its onchain Franklin OnChain U.S. Government Money Fund (FOBXX) for cash management and securities-lending collateral, resolving a conflict with Rule 17f-2 custody provisions written for physical securities. The relief swaps vault-era procedures for digital controls across 12 conditions, giving FOBXX's $726.6M blockchain-recorded shares a path into mainstream fund operations with hourly NAV and intraday settlement.

Coinbase Wins Abu Dhabi License for Tokenized Securities Hub (1 minute read)

Coinbase received a Financial Services Permission from Abu Dhabi Global Market's FSRA to build an international tokenization hub, clearing it to arrange and custody tokenized securities backed 1:1 by underlying shares for eligible investors outside the US. Holders will need only a wallet rather than a brokerage or correspondent-banking relationship, though voting and redemption rights are limited to vested holders and every transfer stays subject to sanctions screening with wallet-level freeze capability.

SEC clears Franklin Templeton funds to use onchain BENJI system for cash management (1 minute read)

The SEC's Division of Investment Management issued a no-action letter clearing Franklin Templeton's traditional registered funds to hold shares of its onchain BENJI money market fund for cash management, waiving certain Rule 17f-2 physical-vault custody requirements under the 1940 Act. The decision lets conventional mutual funds and ETFs tap the fund's faster settlement and more frequent pricing, signaling regulators now treat blockchain book-entry records as equivalent to legacy custody arrangements.

Analysis: Why Revolut Survived and the Metaverse Didn't, a Tale of Financial Fashions (1 minute read)

Lex argues that financial "metas" like ICOs, NFTs, and AI agents can pull in billions before any real economy shows up, and offers five tests to tell a passing fashion from durable infrastructure: external revenue, miracle count, chokepoints, boring prices, and clock speed. The case study is Virtuals, which peaked at $16.5M in monthly fees before collapsing more than 90% in four months even as the tech kept working. The distinction that matters is whether value rests on continued attention or survives on real customers, cash flows, and distribution.
