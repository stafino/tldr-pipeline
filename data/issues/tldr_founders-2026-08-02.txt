Function Health nets $450M from General Catalyst to prove preventive care is cheaper than waiting to get sick (1 minute read)

Function Health raised $450M from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund, a non-dilutive vehicle that ties capital to customer growth rather than equity, making it one of that fund's largest deals to date. The Austin startup bundles 160-plus lab tests and imaging into a $365-a-year membership and now claims 500,000 members and 100M completed tests since 2023. The bet is that catching disease early beats treating it late, in a system where 90% of the $4.9T spent annually goes toward conditions often detectable years before symptoms appear.
