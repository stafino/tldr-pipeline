Megadeals took 87.5% of US venture dollars, and the rest of the market is priced by vintage (1 minute read)

Megadeals took 87.5% of US venture dollars in the first half of 2026, and on the secondaries market the year of a company's last round now matters more than its business. Companies that last priced in 2026 trade at no discount, while 2021 and 2022 vintages sit 54% to 59% below, because a stale round leaves buyers no fresh reference point to argue against. Capital One's $5.2B purchase of Brex, roughly 58% under its $12.3B pandemic peak, is that secondary discount paid in cash.

How a $250 million acquisition collapsed into allegations of fraud and forged signatures (1 minute read)

The $9 Billion Bet on Forward Deployed Engineers (1 minute read)

Deployment, not model access, is now the real bottleneck in enterprise AI, and forward deployed engineers are the ones closing the gap. Job listings for the role jumped more than 800% from January to September 2025, and AWS, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Anthropic have committed roughly $9B to implementation-focused teams. The model only scales if fieldwork becomes product, so each deployment leaves behind reusable software rather than starting from zero. Otherwise you have built a consultancy, not a startup.
