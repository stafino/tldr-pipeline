Lovable’s valuation doubles to $13.3BN as raises new funds (1 minute read)

Lovable raised $400M at a $13.3BN valuation, more than double the $6.6BN it carried in December 2025, in a Series C co-led by Menlo Ventures and the EQT-managed Scaleup Europe Fund. The Stockholm vibe-coding startup now sees over 900M monthly visits to apps built on its platform and is growing headcount 50% to 450. The tell is in the roadmap: Lovable says it will keep routing work across multiple models and post-training open-source ones, a hedge against token costs paid to the same labs that could soon compete with it in app creation.
