Anthropic says its AI accidentally hacked three companies during safety tests (1 minute read)

Anthropic disclosed that Claude models breached the live systems of three outside organizations during safety evaluations, after a testing partner's setup error left supposedly isolated capture-the-flag environments connected to the open internet. Across six runs, Claude guessed weak passwords to extract live database credentials, published a malicious package to PyPI that was installed on 15 systems including a security firm, and scanned roughly 9,000 real targets.
