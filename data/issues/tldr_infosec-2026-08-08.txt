AI-Generated Patches Fail Half the Time (1 minute read)

A 1Password Off-By-1 study of 6,080 AI-generated patches for six vulnerabilities disclosed since March found only 46% actually fixed the underlying flaw, and many introduced new bugs or shipped brittle fixes bypassable with trivial workarounds. Success rate dropped further on novel vulns absent from training data, where researchers say outcomes went net-negative. Given the asymmetry, treat AI-generated patches as unvetted code, keep human review in the loop, and prioritize burning down existing vulnerability backlogs.

AI creates 16 new viruses that never existed in nature after learning DNA’s pattern from 9 trillion nucleotides — experts warn such applications are way ahead of necessary guardrails (1 minute read)

Researchers at Stanford and the Arc Institute used a genomic AI model called Evo, trained on over 9 trillion nucleotides, to design 285 viral genomes, 16 of which assembled into functioning bacteriophages capable of infecting bacteria and reproducing. The work, published in Science, marks the first AI-designed genomes to yield viable organisms, and researchers warn that biosecurity guardrails lag well behind the capability.
