New Passkey Attacks Can Recover Synced Private Keys or Bypass Phishing-Resistant MFA (1 minute read)

You Don’t Need to Hack a Company. Sometimes They’ll Email You Their Secrets. (2 minute read)

Security researcher Cory Solovewicz bought noreply.net and noreply.us and received over 400,000 misdirected emails from roughly 6,200 root domains, including test credentials, employee and customer data, injury reports, and more than 28,000 attachments. The lesson for defenders is that "noreply" and deleted-user addresses are not dead ends. Any external domain your automated systems email can be registered and configured as a catch-all, so audit outbound mail destinations and keep sending domains you control from lapsing.

Microsoft Entra ID has a big change coming for Windows Hello for Business and macOS PSSO (1 minute read)

Microsoft is updating Entra ID to let Windows Hello for Business and macOS Platform SSO function as standalone MFA factors, rather than requiring a separate second factor after passwordless sign-in. The change lets phishing-resistant device credentials satisfy MFA policies on their own, simplifying Conditional Access design for endpoints that already enroll hardware-bound keys. Microsoft plans to begin the rollout later this year.

Stealthium Targets Security Blind Spots in AI Accelerators and Neo-Clouds (1 minute read)

Stealthium launched a security tool that detects compromise of AI accelerators and neo-clouds like CoreWeave and Nebius, where traditional CPU-centric tooling has no visibility into high-speed accelerator video memory. Because Stealthium cannot see inside the accelerators directly, it runs an agent in the customer's own infrastructure trained to spot subtle signals of a compromised neo-cloud, addressing a supply-chain blind spot the recent Januscape malware could have exploited.
