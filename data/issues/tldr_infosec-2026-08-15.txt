How Figma Stays Ahead of Vulnerabilities with Agents (5 minute read)

Figma runs security agents at three stages of its SDLC—code generation, pull request review, and auditing of a decade-old monorepo—all following one shared policy that the agents help refine automatically. PR review anchors the system because its improvement loop is fastest: authors thumbs-up/down each finding to track precision, while replaying known-buggy commits measures recall. Figma runs Claude Code (Opus 4.8, xhigh) and Codex (GPT-5.6, high) in parallel because they miss different bugs, at roughly $0.50 median spend per PR.
