You’re calling the same AI API thousands of times: Why redundant requests are driving hidden AI costs (3 minute read)

The author argues that enterprise AI budgets leak not from model choice but from redundant computation, citing a support system that made 50 identical frontier-model calls to answer the same return-policy question 50 times.
