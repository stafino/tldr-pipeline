Three Critical VMware Flaws Allow Auth Bypass, Code Execution, and VM Escape (1 minute read)

Long-Lived Vulnerability in Microsoft Secure Boot (1 minute read)

ESET researchers found that Microsoft's Secure Boot has been trivially bypassable for 13 of its 14 years, tracing the flaw to 11 defective firmware shims, at least one dating to 2013, that Microsoft kept signing after their vulnerabilities were known. Because Microsoft oversees shim signing and never revoked the compromised images, even novice attackers can circumvent UEFI-level firmware protection across Windows and Linux devices.
