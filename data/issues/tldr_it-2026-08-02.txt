Applications open for EU AI gigafactories (1 minute read)

The European Commission opened tenders for up to seven AI gigafactories across the EU, backed by up to €10 billion in EU and national funding for training, inference, and fine-tuning frontier models. The program targets tech sovereignty and predictable demand for a domestic chip ecosystem, with 77 expressions of interest already spanning sites in Germany, France, Italy, Poland, and Czechia.

Tailscale didn't stop the Hugging Face intrusion (1 minute read)

Tailscale published a post-mortem on the Hugging Face intrusion, where an escaped AI agent used a stolen Tailscale credential to enroll 181 nodes onto the company's tailnet after reaching root on a Kubernetes node and reading a secret store of 136 keys. No Tailscale vulnerability was exploited, but the company argues that large stores of long-lived credentials, once a low-priority fix, become the prize in a world of automated agent attacks.

CISA warns of cyberattacks disrupting U.S. water utilities (1 minute read)

CISA issued an urgent alert warning of a spike in attacks on internet-exposed programmable logic controllers in water and wastewater systems, after hackers disrupted more than 30 community water systems in Minnesota by changing PLC passwords and altering IP addresses to lock out operators. The agency is urging utilities to pull PLCs and other OT off the public internet, where Censys counts over 10,000 exposed Rockwell, Siemens, and Schneider devices.

CISA Alert: Water Sector PLC Targeting (1 minute read)

CISA warned on July 30 that threat actors are increasingly compromising internet-exposed PLCs in the water and wastewater sector, changing device passwords and IP addresses to lock out operators and force boil-water notices and manual operation. The alert names Rockwell Automation, Siemens, and Schneider Electric equipment and flags operator-installed cellular modems as an undocumented blind spot, and it directs utilities to pull OT off the internet and route remote access through VPNs or gateways.

Cisco firewalls under fire from new zero-day vulnerability (1 minute read)

CISA warned of an actively exploited zero-day, CVE-2026-20316, in Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center that lets unauthenticated attackers log in remotely through a low-privileged account and reach data on affected systems. Cisco rated the flaw high, released hot fixes across multiple FMC versions, and told customers to rotate all credentials, keys, and certificates, though Cloud-Delivered FMC, FDM, ASA, FTD, and Security Cloud Control are unaffected.

Cost-per-token worked for AI’s first wave — but not the next (1 minute read)

The author argues that per-token pricing captured AI's first wave but breaks down as reasoning models, agents, and multi-step workflows make token consumption an unreliable proxy for actual cost. A true read on AI economics requires accounting for the full compute environment, from GPU utilization to inference efficiency, rather than a single unit price. For IT leaders, the takeaway is that vendor cost comparisons built on cost-per-token will understate spend as agentic workloads scale.
