Recent SonicWall Vulnerabilities Exploited in Ransomware Attacks (1 minute read)

The INC Ransomware group has emerged as the most active actor exploiting two SonicWall SMA1000 flaws, CVE-2026-15409 (CVSS 10) and CVE-2026-15410, which let unauthenticated attackers tunnel to restricted services and escalate to root. Patched July 14 and added to CISA's KEV catalog the same day, the bugs were exploited as zero-days since June 22, and INC has since listed US and government victims on its leak site.

US Water Cyberattacks Extend Beyond Minnesota to at Least 6 Other States (1 minute read)

A cyber campaign against US water and wastewater operators has spread beyond Minnesota to at least seven states, including Michigan, Georgia, and South Dakota, with federal investigators and a Minnesota Fusion Center report tying the activity to Iran-linked hacking groups. Most facilities reported no operational impact and safe drinking water, but the OT-focused breach at more than 30 Minnesota sites underscores exposure across enterprise SCADA and ICS deployments in critical infrastructure.

AI’s measurement crisis is over. The translation crisis is next (3 minute read)

The author argues the 2025 "AI ROI crisis" was a measurement failure, not an AI failure: MIT's GenAI Divide report counted a pilot as a loss unless it showed KPI impact within six months, so projects that were never instrumented registered as failures despite $30-40B in enterprise spend. The fix is problem selection, not better models, which explains 2026's pivot to employee-facing agents in support, sales, and IT help desks where trusted metrics already exist.

Introducing the Billable Usage API: programmatic cost visibility for Cloudflare (1 minute read)

Cloudflare launched a Billable Usage API for self-serve accounts, a single endpoint that returns usage and cost broken down by product and service period across Workers, R2, D1, Workers AI, Vectorize, Images, and Stream. Column names map to the FOCUS FinOps spec, so teams already ingesting cost data from other providers can pull Cloudflare spend into the same toolchain and give automated agents programmatic visibility into what they are spending.
