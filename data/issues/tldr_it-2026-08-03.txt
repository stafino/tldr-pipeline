Google Password Manager Attacks Could Let Malware Hijack Passkey-Protected Accounts (1 minute read)

Palo Alto Networks' Unit 42 detailed three post-compromise techniques, dubbed Pass-ta-key, against Chrome's Google Password Manager on Windows, letting malware already on a device silently obtain valid authentication assertions or extract the Security Domain Secret that decrypts a victim's synced passkeys. None of the paths break the underlying cryptography or carry a CVE, but the strongest give attackers reusable access to passkey-protected accounts from their own environment after the initial endpoint compromise.

Attackers exploit N-able N-central flaw to reach managed endpoints (CVE-2026-18577) (1 minute read)

Attackers are exploiting an authentication bypass flaw (CVE-2026-18577) in N-able N-central, the RMM platform widely deployed by MSPs, to hijack admin accounts and reach managed endpoints via the Take Control feature, establishing persistence through CloudFlare tunnels. Huntress reports active exploitation and warns that 55.6% of its partners' reachable N-central servers remain unpatched against a hotfix (2026.3.1.7) that self-hosted customers must apply manually.

N-able Says Attackers Take Over N-central Servers After Initial Fix Proves Incomplete (1 minute read)

N-able confirmed attackers exploited an authentication bypass in its N-central RMM platform to gain remote administrative access, then pivoted through the built-in Take Control tool to install persistent Cloudflare tunnels on managed endpoints. The initial fix was incomplete, so every customer must move to build 2026.3.1.7, released August 2, and separately hunt for and remove the tunnel services, which survive the N-central upgrade.

N-able warns of N-central auth bypass flaw exploited in attacks (1 minute read)

N-able warned that attackers are actively exploiting CVE-2026-18577, an authentication bypass in its N-central RMM platform that enables administrative account takeover across both hosted and on-premises servers. The flaw stems from an incomplete patch for an earlier bypass, and because N-central is the tool MSPs use to manage large multi-OS fleets, a compromise lets attackers pivot well beyond N-able's direct customers.

CISA Alert: Water Sector PLC Targeting (1 minute read)

CISA warned on July 30 that threat actors are increasingly compromising internet-exposed PLCs in the water and wastewater sector, changing device passwords and IP addresses to lock out operators and force boil-water notices and manual operation. The alert names Rockwell Automation, Siemens, and Schneider Electric equipment and flags operator-installed cellular modems as an undocumented blind spot, and it directs utilities to pull OT off the internet and route remote access through VPNs or gateways.

Iran suspected of conducting cyberattacks on US water suppliers in 45 municipalities — small towns mostly targeted, with utilities switching to manual control (1 minute read)

Water utilities in 45 US municipalities across seven states, mostly small towns like Braham, Minnesota, reported cyberattacks on their control systems, with officials and the FBI suspecting Iranian state actors and no financial demands made. Several utilities have fallen back to manual control while they assess aging, internet-connected SCADA systems, exposing how thin the OT security budgets are at the small municipalities that run critical infrastructure.
