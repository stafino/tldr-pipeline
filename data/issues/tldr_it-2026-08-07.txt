New WordPress Pre-Auth XSS Could Lead to PHP Code Execution - Patch ASAP (1 minute read)

The fix landed August 6 with backports through the 4.7 branch and automatic delivery to sites running background updates, so IT teams should confirm those updates applied and manually patch anything older.

Deepfakes are targeting your executives. Here’s what actually works (3 minute read)

US cloud 'kill switch' is as dangerous as ransomware, European businesses fear (1 minute read)

A Proton survey of 1,500 firms in the UK, France, and Germany found that 74% are worried about a US government-imposed cloud "kill switch," ranking the risk roughly on par with ransomware. Amazon DynamoDB now supports real-time vector search at any scale (1 minute read)

For shops already standardized on DynamoDB, this removes the dedicated vector database, its sync pipeline, and its licensing costs, folding retrieval for RAG and agentic memory into the same serverless, pay-per-request infrastructure.

The authors introduce NutriScan, a static and dynamic analysis framework, and argue that macOS's fragmented permission mechanisms let undisclosed tracking persist and complicate Apple's app vetting.
