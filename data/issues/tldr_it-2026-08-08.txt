Progress Kemp LoadMaster Flaw Hits CISA KEV After 792 Reported Exploit Attempts (1 minute read)

CISA added a critical command injection flaw in Progress Kemp LoadMaster (CVE-2026-8037, CVSS 9.6) to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog after KEVIntel logged 792 exploitation attempts from 65 IPs across 18 countries in 41 days. The unauthenticated bug allows arbitrary code execution on the load balancer appliance, and FCEB agencies must patch by August 10 under BOD 26-04.

Atlassian Rovo Can Be Tricked Into Sending Jira and Confluence Data to Attackers (1 minute read)

Two security firms independently showed Atlassian's Rovo assistant can be tricked into collecting Jira and Confluence data a signed-in user can access and sending it to an outside server, via a poisoned uploaded file (PromptArmor) or a crafted rovoChatPrompt link (Varonis's RovoBlast). Atlassian fixed the link flaw server-side on July 8, but the content-borne prompt-injection path leaves no patch, only scoping which apps and groups can use Rovo.

Metabase SQLi zero-day exploited in customer data-theft attacks (1 minute read)

Metabase disclosed that attackers exploited a previously unknown, unauthenticated SQL injection flaw rated CVSS 10.0 to breach customer instances on its Metabase Cloud SaaS platform, with Framework and Tally among the confirmed victims. Self-hosted deployments on versions 1.58 and above remain vulnerable until manually patched, and Metabase is urging those customers to upgrade, revoke active sessions, and rotate credentials for all connected databases.
