CISA: SonicWall SMA1000 flaws now exploited by ransomware gangs (1 minute read)

CISA added two recently patched SonicWall SMA1000 flaws, including a maximum-severity SSRF bug (CVE-2026-15409 and CVE-2026-15410), to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog and now flags both as targeted by ransomware gangs. Enterprises, government agencies, and MSSPs running the remote-access gateway should apply SonicWall's July hotfix immediately, as Shadowserver still tracks over 380 exposed appliances and threat actors have exploited the bugs since June 22.

Serving the most critical missions: Cloudflare for Government achieves FedRAMP Class D (High) Certified status (1 minute read)

Cloudflare for Government achieved FedRAMP High authorization, sponsored by NIST and up from the Moderate authorization it held since 2022. The company is now building on those controls to pursue U.S. Department of Defense Impact Level 4 authorization, positioning its global network to serve federal agencies and defense contractors handling controlled unclassified data alongside AWS and Azure's government clouds.

New Passkey Attacks Can Recover Synced Private Keys or Bypass Phishing-Resistant MFA (1 minute read)

Researchers at SpecterOps, Unit 42, and Dirk-jan Mollema demonstrated three separate ways to defeat passkey protections without breaking their cryptography, including recovering private keys for Google Password Manager synced passkeys and impersonating privileged Entra ID users while satisfying phishing-resistant MFA. Microsoft patched the related Windows logging flaw (CVE-2026-34348, CVSS 6.5) and applied Entra-side mitigations, but its own migration guidance still describes passkeys as replay-resistant.

LexisNexis shuts down services after suspicious activity on servers (1 minute read)

LexisNexis took its Nexis Diligence, Metabase API, and Newsdesk services offline after detecting unusual activity on servers run by an unnamed third-party vendor, and is now rebuilding the affected systems in a new environment with help from a forensic firm. The outage cuts off due-diligence, media-monitoring, and data-feed tools that compliance, PR, and enterprise integration teams rely on, marking the third security incident to hit the data-analytics provider since March 2025.

New Jersey, Alabama Join States Targeted in Water Cyberattacks (1 minute read)

At least 12 US states have now reported cyberattacks on their water and wastewater facilities since late July, with New Jersey and Alabama the latest to confirm intrusions into OT and industrial control systems tied to Iranian hackers targeting Rockwell Automation devices. Impact has been limited so far, but CISA is urging the water sector to harden OT, and New York has committed over $9M in grants to shore up utility cybersecurity.

Framework loses customer data in Metabase zero-day attack (1 minute read)

Framework disclosed that attackers exploited a zero-day in Metabase, the open-source business intelligence tool, to access a database containing customer names, email addresses, and shipping details. The breach adds Metabase to the growing list of self-hosted analytics and BI platforms drawing active exploitation, pushing IT teams to audit internet-facing deployments and confirm that patch timelines keep pace with vendor advisories.

ClusterNetworkPolicy in GKE: Balancing control and autonomy for your microservices (2 minute read)

Google added ClusterNetworkPolicy (CNP) to GKE, a cluster-wide, non-bypassable network security resource from the Kubernetes SIG-Policy Working Group that uses a tiered admin/namespace/baseline hierarchy to resolve the policy conflicts that plague standard namespace-scoped NetworkPolicy. Platform and security teams can now enforce compliance mandates and a default deny-all zero-trust posture centrally via RBAC, while developers still write their own application policies without overriding the guardrails.

Cyber vulnerability sweep picks up Royal Navy drones sending data to China (1 minute read)

A UK government cyber vulnerability sweep found that drones operated by the Royal Navy were transmitting data to servers in China, exposing a supply-chain risk in fielded hardware. The finding pushes defense and enterprise buyers to audit the firmware and network behavior of imported IoT and edge devices, since vendor-level data exfiltration can pass procurement checks that only vet the hardware.

Microsoft’s PostgreSQL alternative, HorizonDB: Worth the wait? (2 minute read)

Microsoft's HorizonDB, its cloud-native PostgreSQL alternative built on disaggregated compute and storage, has sat in public preview for nine months with no GA date, even as AWS Aurora, Google AlloyDB, Databricks Lakebase, and Snowflake Postgres already ship AI-ready Postgres services. Analysts argue CIOs with urgent AI workloads have little reason to wait, since an undefined GA, pricing, and support roadmap makes mission-critical planning impossible. Azure-committed shops staging new AI projects are the narrow exception.

A researcher bought noreply.net. Companies started sending him secrets. (1 minute read)

A security researcher who owns the placeholder domains noreply.net and noreply.us has received roughly 700 misdirected emails per day, more than 400,000 since December 2024, containing injury reports, account credentials, and other sensitive data companies assumed were going nowhere. Presented at Defcon, the finding is a reminder that internal systems routing mail to noreply-style domains can leak confidential information to whoever happens to own them.
