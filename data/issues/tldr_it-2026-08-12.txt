Hackers leverage new Microsoft SharePoint exploit in attacks (1 minute read)

Attackers are weaponizing a Rapid7 proof-of-concept exploit for CVE-2026-55040, a critical SharePoint JWT authentication-bypass flaw that lets unprivileged users impersonate site administrators and read or modify files. Microsoft patched it in July 2026 for SharePoint Server 2016 and 2019, but Shadowserver still tracks over 8,500 exposed servers, and CISA has urged IT teams to pull SharePoint off the open internet or place it behind a Layer 7 reverse proxy.

Microsoft patches 400+ vulnerabilities, one zero-day under attack (CVE-2026-68820) (1 minute read)

Microsoft's August 2026 Patch Tuesday fixed more than 400 vulnerabilities, including CVE-2026-68820, an AFD.sys use-after-free that North Korean attackers exploited as a zero-day to escalate to SYSTEM and deploy a kernel-mode rootkit. Three other flaws were publicly disclosed before the patch, so IT teams should prioritize this rollout across Windows fleets rather than wait for the usual staged deployment window.
