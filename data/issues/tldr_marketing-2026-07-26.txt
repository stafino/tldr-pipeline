Kill The Cookie Banner (1 minute read)

The EU Commission's Digital Omnibus reform proposes replacing cookie banners with a browser-level signal that communicates each user's tracking preference automatically, and Google and ad-industry groups are lobbying Member States and the European Parliament to block it. With roughly 90% of users currently clicking "accept" but only about 3% actually wanting to be tracked, a machine-readable consent signal would collapse opt-in rates and force marketers off consent-dependent targeting toward first-party data.
