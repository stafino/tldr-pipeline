ChatGPT Ads adds conversion bidding and stronger measurement (1 minute read)

ChatGPT Ads added conversion-optimized bidding (oCPC), AppsFlyer and Adjust measurement integrations, hashed advanced matching, rolling seven-day average budgets, and bulk campaign management through its Ads API. The release closes the feature gap with Google Ads and Meta Ads, making the platform practical for performance marketers who depend on automation and third-party attribution to defend CAC and ROAS.

Google’s AI search is rapidly becoming the default, new data shows (1 minute read)

Google's AI Overviews now appear in 43% of searches, up from 15% a year ago, while AI Mode visits more than doubled to 279 million by May 2026, per Similarweb. Publishers are absorbing the referral loss as cited AI answers increasingly end the search journey on Google, pushing teams toward proprietary, bottom-of-funnel content and tools like Cloudflare's crawler-blocking marketplace to reclaim leverage.

How AI Search Is Redefining Creator Value for Brands, According to Deloitte Digital’s Jenny Kelly (1 minute read)

Deloitte Digital found that a YouTube creator with roughly 1,200 followers drove outsized visibility in LLM search results for a brand client, while far larger accounts did not surface at all. Jenny Kelly, the firm's head of content, creator, and AI, argues that AI search breaks the follower-count model for evaluating creators and pushes creator strategy from a peripheral channel into the core of the marketing mix.

Snap Closes Out AI Ads Rollout With MCP Server Opening Platform to Third-Party Agents (1 minute read)

Snap opened its ads platform to third-party AI agents through a Model Context Protocol server, making it the fourth major self-serve social platform after Meta, Pinterest, and Google to expose campaign data and controls through the same standard. The move lets marketers query and manage Snap campaigns from external AI assistants, though the server omits the Ad Context Protocol spec that would govern agent-to-agent transactions across platforms.

Google Officially Ends FAQ Rich Results: Should You Remove FAQ Schema? (1 minute read)

Google officially ended support for FAQ rich results, formalizing a phased rollout that began in September 2023 when the feature was restricted to government and health sites. The move is a documentation update rather than a ranking change, so keep existing FAQ schema in place since structured markup still helps AI search engines parse question-and-answer content.
