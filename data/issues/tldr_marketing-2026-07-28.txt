Holiday Creator Content Volume Surged in 2025, But Only Top Brands Converted It Into Attention, Data Shows (1 minute read)

Holiday creator content grew 73% year over year in 2025, per Traackr data on 770,000 creators and 12M posts, but the extra volume mostly failed to convert into attention. The Top 25 brands by Vitality Score earned 57% of their holiday attention from retained creators active in both Q3 and Q4, versus 51% for the rest of the market, and grew attention across every tier while others lost ground at the top. The takeaway for demand-gen teams: creator relationships built before the season outperform in-season spend, which peaked at a $1,750 median December payout.

How AEO drives higher-intent site visitors than other channels (1 minute read)

AEO-referred traffic still makes up under 1% of site visits but converts 3x-15x better than traditional search, per November 2025 Microsoft Clarity data. ChatGPT referrals close at 11.4% versus 5.3% for organic on ecommerce sites, and Copilot's subscription conversion ran 15x higher than search, outpacing direct and social. Answer engines pre-qualify buyers by matching them to your content as the answer rather than one of ten links, so demand-gen teams should start scoring AEO traffic in the CRM to prove its quality before reallocating budget.
