Google testing a way to ease email signups (1 minute read)

Google is testing an experimental Email Verification Protocol (EVP) in Chrome that confirms a user controls an email address using cryptographic proof from their email provider, removing the OTP or magic-link step that interrupts signup flows. For marketers, it cuts friction between interest and conversion while filtering out typos, fake accounts, and disposable addresses before they hit CRM and marketing automation, improving first-party data quality.

Qatar Airways Outperformed Higher-Volume Travel Brands on Instagram by Prioritizing Engagement Over Content Output (1 minute read)

Qatar Airways generated €12.8M in Instagram earned media value from just 1,307 pieces of creator content in H1 2026, ranking fifth among Europe's top 100 travel brands and beating rivals that published several times as much, per Kolsquare. The driver was a 20.2% engagement rate, versus 6.4% for category leader Disneyland Paris, which needed roughly 16 times the content to produce 2.4 times the EMV. The takeaway for creator programs: a smaller roster of high-engagement partnerships can outperform sheer posting volume on EMV.
