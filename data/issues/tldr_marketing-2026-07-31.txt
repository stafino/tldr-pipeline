Which AI actually reads your site? Two months of LLM traffic, measured (5 minute read)

Evil Martians instrumented evilmartians.com for two months and logged roughly 268,000 agent requests to test whether GEO tactics actually work. ChatGPT fetches rendered HTML and almost never touches Markdown, while Claude Code requests Markdown 76% of the time through content negotiation, so a single format bet leaves half your AI traffic unserved. llms.txt got pulled mostly by unrelated bots, and their own hidden AI-hint div drew zero hits, findings only visible because the tracking ran in server-side middleware where JS-free crawlers can be measured.

Canva Built a 90-Million-Backlink Empire. Does It Survive AI Search? (1 minute read)

Canva built a backlink empire of 90M links, and Foundation went back to test whether it survives AI search. The finding: Canva is the cited answer for nearly every "make me an X design" query, but it wins none of the comparison queries where buyers weigh tools against each other. The lesson for marketers is that the landing-page-per-use-case playbook that won SEO now trains LLMs to name your brand, while the missing comparison content cedes citations to competitors right at the decision point.

UTM conventions that stop your analytics turning to mush (3 minute read)

UTM parameters have no schema and nothing validates them, so most broken analytics dies not from a bug but from three people tagging links slightly differently for six months until Instagram splits across three rows you cannot merge. Adopt a convention that takes ten minutes: lowercase everything, use hyphens instead of spaces or underscores, date every campaign (black-friday-2026), and never tag internal links, which start a new session and overwrite the original referrer that told you the visitor came from your newsletter.

How to measure marketing when AI owns discovery (1 minute read)

As AI conversations absorb more top-of-funnel discovery, customers now evaluate products without ever hitting your site, leaving traffic-centric analytics blind to demand that AI recommendations create. The fix is to track brand-name search volume and direct traffic in Google Search Console, measure share-of-voice across the Reddit, YouTube, and LinkedIn sources AI models cite, and adopt 30- to 90-day multi-touch attribution so early non-converting exposures get credit. The visitors who do arrive will be later-stage, so weight assisted conversions and deeper content consumption over raw page views.

Case study: How to design a referral program (1 minute read)

Referral programs work best when they lean on non-monetary, altruistic motivations rather than cash incentives, according to a Science Says case study. The stronger lever is doubling down on users who have already referred someone, since past referrers are the most likely to refer again. For demand-gen teams, that means designing rewards around status and helping peers, then concentrating follow-up asks on the subset of advocates already driving your referral volume.

Influencer Marketing Didn't Blow Up, Five Years of Deal Data Shows It Matured Instead (5 minute read)

Influencer marketing didn't spike into a bubble. A new G&B Digital Management and Bobbie study, drawing on five years of G&B's proprietary deal records, argues the channel matured through three structural shifts: rising contract values, compressing deal timelines, and a growing share of repeat brand-creator relationships. For demand-gen teams, the takeaway is that creator pricing now supports benchmarking. Equivalent creators once quoted the same brand anywhere from $2,500 to $50,000 for one post, and that pricing chaos is what's fading.

SEO Tip: Track the changes your competitors are making on G2 / Capterra for AEO (5 minute read)

Competitors are rewriting their G2, Capterra, and Software Advice profiles for AEO, and Ahrefs "Page Inspect" lets you see the exact content diffs by comparing the current page against a snapshot 6-12 months back. The HubSpot example shows the pattern: features restructured as heading:text pairs plus an "Outcome" section, which hands LLMs clean, citable chunks to lift when someone asks ChatGPT or Perplexity for the best tool in a category. A citation from a third-party domain carries more weight than the same claim on your own site, so track category placement too, not just the description.

What Actually Drives Inbound Job Leads on LinkedIn (5 minute read)

A marketer who started 2026 with ~3,500 LinkedIn followers and no prior organic content documented 70 inbound recruiter and hiring-manager messages in six months, 13 of them real roles at the right level, location, and pay. Inbound conversations put her in five late-stage interview processes, while 148 applications with the same resume in the same market produced three. Her read: content is the push that gets you into hiring feeds, but profile findability in recruiter search is the pull, and inbound leads are the only KPI that measures whether the right people act.
