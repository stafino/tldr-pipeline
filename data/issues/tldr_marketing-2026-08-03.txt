I Was Wrong About Marketing ROI. Here’s What $100,000,000+ in Sponsorship Sales Taught Me. (1 minute read)

After analyzing years of data across $120M+ in SaaStr sponsorships, the author reverses an earlier position: marketing compounds for companies that already have momentum, not for those trying to spend their way out of a slowdown. The higher-ROI move is capturing existing demand, meeting prospects already in the funnel and accelerating deals in motion, rather than manufacturing demand for a product the market has cooled on. The tactical read is to concentrate spend where product-market fit is strong and treat marketing cuts during a slump as a signal to fix the product, not just the campaign.

Optimizing your B2B brand for autonomous AI shoppers (1 minute read)

As B2B buyers increasingly delegate product research to AI agents, the signals that earn a citation diverge from classic SEO. Agents pull from structured data, third-party review sites, and pages with clear proprietary evidence rather than keyword-optimized top-of-funnel content. Marketers who want to be surfaced should publish specific, verifiable claims, keep product and pricing details machine-readable, and seed the review platforms and communities that AI shoppers treat as trusted sources.

Relevance Engineering: The New Discipline for the AI Agent Era (1 minute read)

AI bot traffic rose 187% during 2025 while human web traffic grew just 3.1%, and agentic traffic (autonomous systems retrieving and acting on a user's behalf) jumped 7,851%, per Cloudflare. As agents research, compare, and check out inside their own interfaces, discovery and purchase collapse into one invisible interaction that breaks click-based attribution. Brainlabs argues the counter is "relevance engineering": structuring content as machine-readable context so an AI selects your brand for the specific user it serves, rather than publishing more top-of-funnel volume.
