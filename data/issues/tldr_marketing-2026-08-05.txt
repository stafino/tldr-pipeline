Snapchat offers third-party AI ad integration (1 minute read)

Snapchat launched its Snapchat Ads MCP Server, an official connection that feeds Snap Ads API data into Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini so advertisers can build and refine campaigns from inside those tools. Connections are read-only at launch and require per-agent admin authorization, but planned write access would open the door to agentic campaign creation, following Meta, TikTok, and Pinterest into third-party MCP integration.

Shopify says AI search is driving more traffic and sales, not replacing Google (1 minute read)

Shopify credited its Q2 earnings beat to AI search, reporting that AI-driven traffic and orders to merchant stores tripled year-over-year while revenue rose 36% to $3.6B. Unlike publishers losing clicks to AI summaries, Shopify says agents make multiple calls into its structured catalog to match products against specific buyer intent, and traditional search still grew to hold roughly a third of storefront sessions.

LinkedIn Is Cracking Down on AI Slop. Here’s What That Means for Your Strategy (1 minute read)

LinkedIn announced algorithm changes on May 20 that suppress AI-generated posts flagged as generic beyond a user's immediate network, cutting the broader recommendation reach that makes the platform valuable for brand awareness and thought leadership. AI-assisted content still qualifies for distribution if it carries original perspective or expertise, so brands pairing AI efficiency with genuine subject-matter depth gain an edge as detection tightens.
