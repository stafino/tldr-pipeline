How Reddit moderators are battling AI-powered astroturfing ad campaigns that exploit the platform's trusted reputation to promote skincare and other products (Mia Sato/The Verge) (1 minute read)

Reddit moderators are fighting a wave of AI-powered astroturfing campaigns that plant fake user recommendations to promote skincare and other products, exploiting the platform's reputation as a source of authentic peer advice. The tactic threatens the trust that makes Reddit valuable to marketers, and it raises the stakes for brands weighing whether covert AI-generated word-of-mouth is worth the reputational and moderation risk.
