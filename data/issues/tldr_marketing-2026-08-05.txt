TIME Is Serving AI Bots a Different Website, with Ads Built In (1 minute read)

TIME now serves AI crawlers a different site than humans, handing bots like ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, and OpenAI's search crawler a stripped 13KB markdown copy with ads baked in via ad-tech vendor Mobian, while browsers and Googlebot get the full 303KB page. Each bot fetch logs a fresh ad impression counted in tokens rather than pageviews, signaling a new inventory model as publishers monetize AI-assistant traffic that no human ever sees.
