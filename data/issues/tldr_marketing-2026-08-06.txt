Investigating Click Behaviors On Google Search Result Pages That Produce an AI Overview (1 minute read)

A study of one month of browsing data from 900 US adults found that clicks to sources cited in Google's AI Overviews happen in only about 1% of AI Overview visits, and Overviews are linked to fewer clicks and more session-ending searches. Query attributes like length, a leading question word, and a noun-verb pair make an Overview more likely, giving SEO teams concrete signals for which pages face the steepest click loss.

Meta ads introduces stablecoin payment options (1 minute read)

Meta introduced third-party stablecoin payment options for creators and advertisers, letting them fund campaigns and receive payouts in cryptocurrency after abandoning its own Libra stablecoin project. The move gives advertisers a lower-fee, faster-settling alternative to card rails for cross-border ad spend, and reopens crypto payments on Meta's platforms through outside providers rather than a first-party coin.

TIME Is Serving AI Bots a Different Website, with Ads Built In (1 minute read)

TIME now serves AI crawlers a different site than humans, handing bots like ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, and OpenAI's search crawler a stripped 13KB markdown copy with ads baked in via ad-tech vendor Mobian, while browsers and Googlebot get the full 303KB page. Each bot fetch logs a fresh ad impression counted in tokens rather than pageviews, signaling a new inventory model as publishers monetize AI-assistant traffic that no human ever sees.
