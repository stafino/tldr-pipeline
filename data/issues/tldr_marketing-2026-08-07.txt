Consumers Forget Brands Quickly, and AI Often Can’t Help Them Recover Them, Study Finds (1 minute read)

Only 17% of consumers can confidently recall the last three ads they saw after 24 hours, and 31% now turn to an AI chatbot to recover a forgotten brand name, per a new Adobe survey of 1,002 US consumers. Just half of those chatbot users say AI names the right brand even half the time, leaving marketers exposed on both recall and AI-assisted rediscovery.

How to Optimize Your YouTube Channel for AI Visibility (1 minute read)

YouTube mentions showed a 0.737 correlation with AI visibility in Foundation's study of 8,566 B2B SaaS keywords, the strongest factor measured, ahead of Domain Rating and backlinks. YouTube also landed in Google's top 10 for 80.2% of "demo" searches, 22.5% of "best" searches, and 20% of "vs" searches. The tactical takeaway for B2B teams is that a small library of videos built around buyer questions, with clean titles, chapters, and timestamps, keeps surfacing across Google, YouTube, and AI answers without needing millions of views.
