Signed up for Klaviyo? Dozens of advertisers may have seen your password (1 minute read)

Klaviyo left its sign-up form misconfigured from at least February 2024 through November 2025, leaking new customers' email addresses and plaintext passwords to third-party trackers from Google, Meta, Microsoft, LinkedIn, HubSpot, and X. The incident, disclosed by researcher Sam Jadali ahead of a Def Con talk, is a reminder that marketing pixels can quietly exfiltrate any data entered on the pages they sit on.
