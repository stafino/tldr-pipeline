Testing ads in ChatGPT (1 minute read)

OpenAI expanded its ChatGPT Ads pilot to the UK, Mexico, Brazil, Japan, and South Korea, after a US test that showed no drop in trust metrics and low ad-dismissal rates. Ads appear only for logged-in adults on the Free and Go tiers, don't influence answers, and keep conversations private from advertisers, giving marketers a new intent-rich placement inside the assistant itself.

Advertisers are trying to influence AI bots with secret ads (1 minute read)

Advertisers are now serving hidden ads to AI crawlers, embedding content designed to poison model outputs and nudge chatbots toward mentioning their brands when users ask for recommendations. As AI search absorbs more top-of-funnel discovery, this LLM-targeted prompt injection points to a new gray-area channel that could distort AI-generated brand mentions and undermine the organic share-of-voice marketers are starting to track.

Facebook is paying controversial creators to produce rage-bait content (1 minute read)

An ABC News Verify investigation found Meta is directly paying several controversial Australian creators through its Content Monetization program, including a white nationalist with neo-Nazi links and a prominent anti-vaxxer whose pages carry content that violates Facebook's own monetization policies. Meta declined to comment on the specific pages, saying it is "not Meta's role to police offensiveness," which leaves brand-safety and adjacency risk on advertisers whose spend funds the same revenue-share pool.

IAB Tech Lab releases new privacy standards (1 minute read)

The IAB Tech Lab released updated privacy standards designed to track evolving state-level laws and standardize how consumer privacy choices are communicated across the ad ecosystem. For marketers, the payoff is cleaner consent signals passed through the programmatic supply chain, reducing the compliance guesswork that has fragmented targeting and measurement as more states enact their own rules.

Snapchat ads pharma ad disclaimer links (1 minute read)

Snapchat is adding pharma-specific ad features, supporting multi-CTA attachments and letting health brands surface required safety disclaimers within in-feed display promotions. The move opens Snapchat to regulated pharma and health advertisers who previously couldn't run compliant creative, giving them a younger-skewing channel with the fair-balance disclosure the FDA requires.

Study highlights Reddit’s influence on purchase decisions (1 minute read)

A new study found that Reddit users in the US and UK increasingly turn to its communities for product advice, often trusting fellow posters more than family or friends. Marketers should treat Reddit as a high-intent research channel worth active participation and monitoring, since AI Overviews and search engines now surface Reddit threads directly in purchase-stage results.

YouTube announces changes to creator monetization (1 minute read)

YouTube is doubling YouTube Partner Program entry requirements to 8,000 watch hours or 20 million Shorts views, effective February 2027, while existing YPP creators stay eligible under current thresholds. The platform is also adding Shorts ad formats that pay creators 45% of revenue when advertisers target five or fewer channels, part of a push to monetize its 200 billion daily Shorts views.

Google Ad and Analytics add more AI tools (1 minute read)

Google added Gemini-powered insights across Google Ads and Google Analytics, including AI Overviews summarizing traffic and sales shifts, personalized insight cards, a competitive benchmarking tool, and text-prompt dashboards that turn raw data into visualizations. Marketers can now query performance data in conversational language to understand impression-share changes and campaign trends, shortening the path from raw metrics to a strategy adjustment.

Meta adds dedicated ad exclusion audiences (1 minute read)

Meta added exclusion-only custom audiences to ad sets, a customer list that can only remove people from targeting and never add them, and can't be converted back to a regular custom audience once created. The use cases are cutting wasted spend on opted-out customers, meeting legal or regulatory targeting obligations, and blocking employees or accidental inclusion as a permanent guardrail.

Strategies & Tactics

How to make people care about your startup (1 minute read)

Founder-led communication is now one of the lowest-cost, highest-leverage ways to earn attention as AI-generated content floods X and LinkedIn and buries generic startup posts. The piece maps three founder archetypes to distinct origin-story angles, arguing that the goal is not to be vulnerable or contrarian in every post but to say something honest and specific enough to cut through the noise. For founders who reach out saying "I have no idea what to say," the fix is turning your actual origin story into a repeatable comms strategy.

Perplexity cites ClickUp 6,474 times. Notion gets 741… Why? (1 minute read)

Roughly half of every AI answer is unsolicited commentary the model adds on its own, and Profound's breakdown shows it clusters in the categories that decide purchases: rationale, comparison tables, rankings, and pricing caveats. Citations and search traffic come from different surfaces, so ranking in Google no longer guarantees you get cited. Notion pulls 5.46M monthly organic visits to ClickUp's 1.77M, yet Perplexity cites ClickUp 6,474 times to Notion's 741. Audit what AI engines say about you, not just whether you appear.

AI search performance KPIs every marketer should track (1 minute read)

Visitors arriving via AI search convert at 4.4x the rate of standard organic traffic, per Semrush, which means a brand can lose 40% of its traffic and still win in AI search while a #1-ranked competitor stays invisible across every AI engine. That gap is why traffic and rankings no longer tell the full story. HubSpot's guide lays out the AI search KPIs that do: visibility, citation share, AI-driven conversions, and revenue impact, plus how to tie each back to pipeline.
