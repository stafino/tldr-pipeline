ChatGPT Citations for SaaS Prompts Give User-Generated Content a Larger Role (2 minute read)

An analysis of roughly 35,000 ChatGPT citation URLs for US B2B SaaS prompts found that user-generated content platforms like Reddit, YouTube, and Quora made up 17.1% of cited domains, more than four times the 4.0% share held by publishers. For SaaS teams tracking AI visibility, community and review ecosystems now sit inside the core information environment, so earning peer discussion matters as much as brand-owned pages and press coverage.
