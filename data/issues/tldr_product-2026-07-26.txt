The AI Productivity Paradox (1 minute read)

The author argues the "AI productivity paradox," where teams ship faster but outcomes stagnate, isn't a paradox at all. Most teams use AI to accelerate the same project model, which was always built to deliver output rather than outcomes, so faster building just means running the wrong direction faster. The takeaway for PMs is that AI widens the gap between strong product companies and everyone else, because the real advantage was always discovery, strategy, and culture, not delivery speed.

TBM 432: Bundling & Unbundling Capabilities (and AI) (1 minute read)

The author argues that roles like "product manager" are just temporary bundles of capabilities, and that AI isn't cleanly replacing any single role but moving capabilities through distinct motions: specialization, diffusion, centralization, embedding, rebundling, elimination, and more. The practical read for PMs is to stop tracking job-title churn and instead ask which specific capabilities are shifting, where they are landing, and whether any are being lost before they have been reproduced elsewhere.
