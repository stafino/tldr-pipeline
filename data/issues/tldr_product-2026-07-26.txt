Anthropic’s first technical PM on token maxing, the jagged edge, and living in the future | Dianne Penn (1 minute read)

Anthropic's first technical PM, Dianne Penn, describes the eval-driven development loop her team uses to ship models, where evals define the product spec before code and act as the primary feedback signal on whether a capability is working. She argues that Claude's "jagged edge," strength on some tasks and unreliability on adjacent ones, is why human judgment stays central to product decisions, and that the PM's job is increasingly to build taste for where the model can and can't be trusted rather than to write requirements.
