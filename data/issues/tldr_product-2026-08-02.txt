This CPO regrets that product management exists | Tom Verrilli (CPO of Whatnot) (1 minute read)

Whatnot CPO Tom Verrilli argues the company built its product org on the premise that product management, as a distinct function, is a cost to minimize rather than a role to scale. His model pushes senior ICs to do discovery and shipping directly, with AI absorbing much of the data-science and coordination work a PM layer traditionally owned. The takeaway for PM leaders is a harder question about where a dedicated PM adds value versus where it inserts a handoff.
