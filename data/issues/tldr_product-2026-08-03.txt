TBM 434: How Maps Can Hide Problems (1 minute read)

The author argues that org maps like team structures, product taxonomies, and goal hierarchies only stay reliable when strategy, structure, technology, and incentives cohere, so a map that reads cleanly can quietly paper over an incoherent org underneath. When eight teams span competing hierarchies with unclear ownership and conflicting funding bets, no single map holds and every switch forces you to reorient. The takeaway for PMs: treat a map that requires constant translation as a signal to refactor the organization, not a skill to master.
