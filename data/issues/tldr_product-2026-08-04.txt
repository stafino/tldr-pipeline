Can AI Agents Simulate A/B Test Outcomes? A Validation Framework for Agentic Experimentation (1 minute read)

This paper asks whether AI agents conditioned on behavioral profiles can simulate A/B test outcomes before spending real traffic, formalizing the idea as a Simulated Randomized Controlled Trial. Validated on 67 historical marketing tests, an off-the-shelf model captured directional signal (0.70 sign overlap) but overshot effect sizes, though a two-phase calibration cut squared prediction error roughly 77x. For PMs, the honest read is pre-vetting candidate treatments to prioritize which bets deserve live traffic, not replacing the experiment itself.

From Information to Delegation: Mapping Human-AI Financial Decision Making (1 minute read)

This paper introduces a framework that measures not just what people ask AI about finances, but how much decision authority they hand over. Applied to 1.5 million ChatGPT and Gemini interactions from 6,304 US and India users, it finds financial services are already a heavy AI use case, but consumers use AI to gather information and shape judgment rather than execute. The delegation gap gives PMs building agentic finance products a baseline: trust in retrieval is here, trust in execution is not.
