A Fresh Definition of The Product Role (1 minute read)

Marty Cagan of SVPG argues that the best recent definition of the product manager role came from an unlikely source: a Benedict Evans piece that barely mentions the word "product." Evans' point is that even as customers gain tools to build their own solutions through vibe-coding and agents, most people don't want to be tool builders, which is exactly the gap the PM role exists to fill. Cagan also treats "how do you define the product role" as an interview Rorschach test, since the answer reveals which operating model a candidate actually worked in.
