Player Perceptions of Generative AI in Games: A Steam Review Analysis (1 minute read)

A study of 508,192 Steam reviews found that games disclosing generative AI use receive lower recommendation rates and more negative sentiment than games using decades-old procedural content generation. Thematic coding of 600 reviews shows players read generative AI as a signal of low developer investment, not added value. The authors argue adoption succeeds only when generative AI solves a player need rather than cutting production costs, a useful frame for any PM weighing where to disclose AI in a shipped product.
