Why NIST Researchers Spent 10 Years Measuring Gravity (2 minute read)

NIST physicist Stephan Schlamminger completed a 10-year effort to remeasure big G, the gravitational constant, arriving at 6.67387 x 10-11 m3/(kg s2) using a torsion balance with a four-cylinder geometry inside a vacuum. The result lands 0.0235 percent below an earlier BIPM measurement it aimed to replicate, and the team found no single error to explain the gap, leaving one of physics' most stubbornly uncertain constants still unresolved.
