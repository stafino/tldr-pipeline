Stanford and Arc Institute Build 16 AI-Designed Viruses That Kill E. Coli (1 minute read)

Stanford and Arc Institute researchers used the Evo 1 and Evo 2 genome language models to design complete bacteriophage genomes from scratch, yielding 16 viable viruses that infected and killed E. coli in the first reported case of generative AI composing a functional genome. The models generated roughly 700,000 candidates seeded from short ΦX174 fragments, of which 285 were synthesized for a 5.6% hit rate, and a cocktail of the designs beat resistance that natural phages could not.
