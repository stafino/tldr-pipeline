Dithered QR Codes (1 minute read)

A developer embedded a low-res, one-bit photo inside a QR code by shrinking each data module to the center of a three-by-three grid and filling the surrounding cells with image pixels, exploiting the fact that scanners only read where the finder patterns say the module centers are. The post walks through the image side, comparing simple thresholding against Bayer and Floyd-Steinberg dithering to preserve midtones without breaking scannability.
