DiffusionGemma Technical Report (5 minute read)

Google released DiffusionGemma, an experimental open-weight model that uses discrete diffusion to generate text in parallel blocks of 256 tokens rather than one token at a time. Fine-tuned from the Gemma 4 mixture-of-experts model on under 10% of its original token budget, it reaches roughly 1,500 output tokens per second on a single H100 while retaining thinking mode, multimodal inputs, and long-context support.

Don't be a meat proxy (1 minute read)

The author argues that pasting an AI's verbatim output into Slack, code reviews, or group chats adds no value, since anyone can query the model themselves faster and with better context. Real contribution comes from reading, validating, and rewriting the response in your own words, which serves as proof you actually did the work rather than acting as a "meat proxy" between the reader and the model.
