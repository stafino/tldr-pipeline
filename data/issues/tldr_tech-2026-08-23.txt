Don't be a meat proxy (1 minute read)

The author argues that pasting an AI's verbatim output into Slack, code reviews, or group chats adds no value, since anyone can query the model themselves faster and with better context. Real contribution comes from reading, validating, and rewriting the response in your own words, which serves as proof you actually did the work rather than acting as a "meat proxy" between the reader and the model.
