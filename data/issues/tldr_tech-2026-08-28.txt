Compression is prediction (1 minute read)

This post explains that data compression and language modeling solve the same underlying problem: predicting the next symbol well enough to encode it in fewer bits. It walks through the building blocks of modern compressors, including transforms like run-length encoding, statistical models, and entropy coders, to show why a better predictor is mathematically equivalent to a better compressor.
